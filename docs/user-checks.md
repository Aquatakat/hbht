# Checks for the user

What only the user can settle: by playing the original in the emulator, by listening, or in full-game testing. Each item says what to do and what to look for, and links to the doc that holds the port's reading. When one is settled, the finding goes in that doc and the item comes off this list.

A check far into the story needn't be played to: Claude writes a save at the place, flags and party it needs into the Controller Pak beside 1.0's ROM, and says which slot holds it ([reference rig](reference-rig.md#setting-up-a-check)). The saves written before Nov 13, 2026 were lost with ares's pak; a check that names one needs it rewritten from the story pak.

## Open

- **Zaratas in the Ugarit palace, before his scene.** Walk up from the palace's arrival (Ugarit O03, the floor below) to Zaratas on the floor above (O03/8) without the scene at O03/2 (`0x04C1`, radius 5) starting, and talk to him. The walk graph's every way up ends about 4.7 from the scene's middle, inside its radius; if 1.0 lets a player pass outside it, 3193 is the bot's, and if not, the relaxation's ([playthrough](systems/playthrough.md#after-stage-9)).
- **A save made while a spell holds a member up.** In 1.0, let Rheda's Endurance raise a member's hit points in a battle, end it with that member low (a few hit points), save at once, and load: does the member load alive, with the same hit points? The port's save keeps each stat's trained and current values and loses what the spell left raised, so Brenna loaded dead from such a save in Shamsuk's Tower ([saves](systems/saves.md#loading-as-the-original-does)).
- **A throw's and an arrow's timing in battle.** The user found them late in the port after Start Early's first stage (the battle stepped without asking the views anything), while the ambient sounds, the music and the casting sounds were right. Under Combat Turns: Original, throw something and loose an arrow side by side with 1.0, and watch when the missile leaves the hand and lands against its sound. If 1.0's are the same, it's 1.0's; if not, the first stage moved them. Tightening them afterwards is the user's choice. The port's reading: the missile leaves at the strike ([combat-actions](systems/combat-actions.md#traps)).

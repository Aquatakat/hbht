# Checks for the user

What only the user can settle: by playing the original in the emulator, by listening, or in full-game testing. Each item says what to do and what to look for, and links to the doc that holds the port's reading. When one is settled, the finding goes in that doc and the item comes off this list.

A check far into the story needn't be played to: Claude writes a save at the place, flags and party it needs into the Controller Pak beside 1.0's ROM, and says which slot holds it ([reference rig](reference-rig.md#setting-up-a-check)). The saves written before Nov 13, 2026 were lost with ares's pak; a check that names one needs it rewritten from the story pak.

## Open

- **Camera: Enhanced in trek and in conversations.** Built Nov 14, 2026 and measured headless, not yet played. With Camera: Enhanced on (or `--trek-camera enhanced` and `--dialogue-camera enhanced`, each of which takes `,Name=value` to tune a number without a rebuild): go through doors into interiors (GwerniaCastle M05's hallway from the courtyard, where his back is to the door) and see whether the arrival frame (beside him or in front three-quarter, at the room's length, where behind him has no room) frames him well, whether the close frames against a wall (2 to 2.9 from him, tried before turning to his front) look right, whether the swing behind him as he sets off (90°/s at most, eased in and out, the open way round) is calm, and whether the follow's settle (a long tail after he stops) feels gradual rather than abrupt; go from GwerniaCastle A07 into A05 and from Port Saiid's A09 out to the Overworld and stand: the arrival view should hold until he moves; walk to a wall, turn your back to it and stop: the view should rest beside him, not over his head; run round GwerniaCastle G01's meeting table, hugging it and then wider, and walk round Port Saiid's E01 cave (map set 2, from the Overworld's B15): whether the camera now turns to stay behind him rather than popping in, and how the quickened follow (up to 150°/s) reads; in the cave, walking where the boom straight behind him would be cut, the walking wedge (round six) should ease the camera to a rear three-quarter within 40° of behind that keeps the full arm, and ease home when behind is whole again (`WedgeWidth=0` turns it off to compare); the pops left in the cave headless are turning back on himself in a passage, the camera swinging round through rock, which the wedge doesn't touch: say whether that's what you see; at the table the furniture rule is on by default (`--trek-camera enhanced,BoomLimits=collision` turns it off), where the boom stays whole passing over the table's box, and in a room of tables (map set 6's E05, from the Overworld's P22), and `BoomLimits=drawn` for comparison (worse in the cave); after talking to someone on Port Saiid's piers, walk off and check the view no longer spins; walk Overworld C15's forest path holding only forward and feel whether the bend's lean is noticed; pass chests, npcs and encounters for the nudges' size; sneak near an encounter for whether the swing onto it is assertive enough or too much; talk to npcs indoors and out, in rooms with a fixed camera too, for the shots' distances, the side chosen, the cut back, and how often a long tale (Baldric's in Terminor) changes shot. The cameras doc's two sections hold the design and the numbers ([trek](systems/cameras.md#the-enhanced-trek-camera-camera-enhanced), [conversations](systems/cameras.md#the-enhanced-conversation-camera-camera-enhanced)). The single (Nov 15, 2026) is now an inside single, seen from the front three-quarter with the room in front of the face, clean in a 16:9 window: talk to someone standing a little apart (a step back from them before pressing A) and look at a single of either talker, then the Port Saiid woman at the corner of Overworld B15, where touching close the single now gives way to over the shoulder. `--dialogue-camera enhanced,SingleAngleMost=50,SingleOuts=1;1.2,SingleAheads=0;0.2,SingleLeads=0;0.15,PunchAhead=0.3,CleanAspect=2.33` tries other values.
- **Movement: Smoother up E05's ramps and over short steep strips.** Built Nov 15, 2026 and run headless, not yet played. With Movement on Smoother, in the Misc interiors' E05 (from the Overworld's P22), run up each of the five ramps to its chest and open it, then run back down: he should slip between the rails at the top and back without catching. At Overworld E15's rock, about (22, −27.8, 11.5), run north onto the grass, straight and slanting, and at M07 about (63, −27.4, 73) over the stone strip there: he should climb it as a step, slower as up any steep slope. Say whether the climb reads well, and whether anything nearby now climbs that shouldn't. [Movement](systems/movement.md#modern-movement) has the rules, and the places the scan turned up.
- **A throw's and an arrow's timing in battle.** The user found them late in the port after Start Early's first stage (the battle stepped without asking the views anything), while the ambient sounds, the music and the casting sounds were right. Under Combat Turns: Original, throw something and loose an arrow side by side with 1.0, and watch when the missile leaves the hand and lands against its sound. If 1.0's are the same, it's 1.0's; if not, the first stage moved them. Tightening them afterwards is the user's choice.

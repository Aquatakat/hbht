# Combat: battle mode (2.5, 2.6, M3)

This is the map of the original's combat that M3 is planned from ([roadmap](../roadmap.md#next)): its code, its states and what it keeps in RAM. Each part gets its detail as the session that ports it reads it. What the player sees is in [engine-behaviour](../engine-behaviour.md#battle-mode). [The port](#the-port) says what's ported so far and by which classes: a battle into its arena and out, with the combat camera and the pause menu's Flee, its turns, walking in the field and skipping, the battle screen, attacking, death and reserves, the enemy AI: walking, fighting, morale and fleeing, winning and losing: the rewards, the post-battle windows, the game over, the deaths and the camp's ambush, and spells, potions and skills in a turn: the held menus, target set B, placed areas, casting, drinking and throwing, the Troubadour, the Healer and the pause menu's Party tab.

Addresses are 1.0's. Names are in the symbol map, prefixed `Combat.`, `Combatant.`, `TurnOrder.`, `ArenaGrid.`, `TargetSet.`, `EnemyAI.`, `AiScores.`, `CombatGui.`, `CombatMenu.`, `CombatMarkers.`, `CombatSpellFx.`, `CombatCamera.`, `CombatRadar.` and so on, all coined but `ProcessCombatCamera`. Fields marked **checked** were read in the combat checkpoints ([rig](../reference-rig.md#checkpoint-library)), one battle against ten rats; the rest are read from the code.

Combat is game mode 2 (`Game.Mode`, `0x800D80C8`). `ScreenFade.Step` (`0x8001F330`) calls `Combat.Frame` (`0x80022BA4`) each frame, and the mode it returns is the next: 2 to stay, 1 trek, 3 the pause menu, 6 the post-battle windows, `0xC` a cut-scene, `0xE` a cinematic, 0 leaves the game.

## In and out

**The setup block** (`0x800D9456`–`0x800D9485`, **checked**: empty in checkpoint 01, filled in 02) is what every source fills, and `BattleSetup` is the port's:

| Address | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `0x800D9456` | 1 when filled; 0 makes the debug build's random test battle |
| `0x800D9458` | 12 entity IDs, 0 ending them (ten rats, `0x026E`): the first four start on the field, the rest are reserves |
| `0x800D9470` | bonus loot: an item, or a chest if its type is 8 |
| `0x800D9472` | the encounter flag (1321 for the rats; the Goblin Ambush's is 978) |
| `0x800D9474` | 1 if winning remembers the monster party's map object; then its index (`0x800D9476`), map set (`0x800D9478`), row and column (`0x800D947A`, `0x800D947C`) |
| `0x800D947E` | the collision byte, 0–2: how the party's placed. 2 is an ambush |
| `0x800D947F` | the arena, 0–27 |
| `0x800D9480` | the animation byte, 0–3: the enemies' spawn set is 4 less |
| `0x800D9481` | can flee |
| `0x800D9482`, `0x800D9484` | the monster party object's flag slot A (`+0x1C`) and its flags (`+0x14`), which `Combat.StateVictory` evaluates (`MapObjects.GateHolds`, `0x800116AC`) before it remembers the party |

- **Filled by** `BattleSetup.FromMonsterParty` (`0x800094B4`: `Wanderer.Tick`, `IMapObjectHost.Talk` and a script's battle op, `0x80050804`), `CampEncounters.Load` (`0x800096DC`: collision byte 2, animation 0, no fleeing, no loot or flag) and the script ops. `Wanderer.Tick` writes collision byte 2 and the animation byte by what the player was doing as it noticed him (3 running, 2 walking, 1 sneaking, else 0); `Talk` writes animation 0 and collision 0 with the player in a cone ahead of the party's body, else 1 (`0x80010AE8`–`0x80010B74`).
- **The enemy list** (`BattleSetup.FillEnemies`, `0x80009374`) is three passes over a party's eight kinds: each kind's least, in order, stopping at the party's most or 12 (`BattleSetup.SureKinds`, `0x80008EC8`); then, unless that reached the most, a `Range(0, most − least)` more of each kind whose most is above its least, in order, stopping the same way, and none at all if it comes to under the party's least (`BattleSetup.ChooseKinds`, `0x800090A4`); then, where the party's average level is below the enemies', each kind loses 0.3, 0.2 or 0.1 of its count by the party's level × 3 over the enemies' (`BattleSetup.TrimByLevel`, `0x800088E4`, the shares at `0x800CD470`), removed from the front, an entry shifted into a removed one's place being passed over. A list come to nothing is one Skeleton (`EntityList[0xBB]`, `0x0263`). So the field is the sure ones first, not a shuffle. `Combat.ForbidFleeForBosses` (`0x8005CCCC`) clears the flee byte for 13 entities (`0x800D2688`), and `Combat.ApplyFleeRuleByFlag` (`0x8005D054`) has the last word by flag ([hardcoded-content](../hardcoded-content.md)).
- **The arena** (`BattleSetup.ChooseArena`, `0x80008698`, for every battle but a script's) is by the terrain of the chunk the player's in (`World.GetTerrain`), through a jump table at `0x800CD400`. A range is a roll between them, inclusive (`Rng.Range`):

  | Terrain | Arena | Terrain | Arena |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 0, 7 | 6–7 | 11 | 23 |
  | 1, 24 | 2–3 | 12 | 22 |
  | 2 | 0–1 | 13, 25 | 27 |
  | 3, 27 | 14–15 | 14 | 24 |
  | 4 | 19 | 15 | 25 |
  | 5, 17 | 4–5 | 16 | 26 |
  | 6 | 18 | 18 | 20 |
  | 8 | 12–13 | 19, 23, 26 | 8–11 |
  | 9 | 17 | 20 | one in two 6–7, else 8–11 |
  | 10 | 16 | 21 | one in two 2–3, else 8–11 |
  | | | 22 | one in two 0–1, else 8–11 |

  A terrain of 28 or more writes nothing, so the last battle's arena stays. A script's arena is its own, above 27 made 0 (`0x80050804`). Nothing but the debug build's random battle reaches arena 21. Terrains 20–22 draw `RollD(1, 2)` first, 1 for the first range. The importer carries the table as a fact on each terrain record (`TerrainRecord.Arenas`, `Aidyn.Rom.Engine.Arenas`).
- **The switch.** `TrekLoop.QueueState(2)` (`0x8001FF04`) fades out; trek's state 2 marks trek to be built again on return (`0x800DB067`) and returns mode 2.
- **`Combat.Init`** (`0x80022D1C`, on the first frame, while `0x800DB0A4` is 1): takes down trek's HUD, allocates the combat struct (`Combat.Reset`) and loads the combat strings, sets map set 16 (`0x800D8414`) and the arena's chunk (`0x800D8416`), and starts the zone engine in mode 1 (`InitZoneEngine(1, 0)`, `Zones.Mode`): trek's chunk streamer, collision, lights, sky and weather, over the Battle arenas, with the zone engine's point at (20, 20) in the arena chunk, the shadows' distance 128, and the battle's song chosen in `Zones.SetMapSet` (one `Below(5)` of the map set's five, or `Below(4)` of four without the Expansion Pak). `Combat.Setup` (`0x8005D15C`) fills the struct: the counts, the arena grid, a combatant and a body for each living party member and enemy on the field, Sholeh moved to the party's side if she's in the first four, the leader, whether there are flee points, the turn order and the flee rules by flag. Then the GUI, a body for each combatant (`Combat.CreateBody`, `0x8005C474`), the first turn's line, the camera on the current combatant's body (`Camera.SetEye`, `GiveCameraToPlayer`, `CombatCamera.Reset`), the first turn (`Combat.StartFirstTurn`, `0x80022C9C`: its line, the radius, the ring and the field, but not the clock), the effects, and state 31 with the fade in, a sixtieth a tick (`0x800CE308`). The Goblin Ambush's flag (978) makes it non-lethal, gives the Shadow two turns and heals the first member. Its `0x800E6850` and `0x800E6860` are `Combat.StateMoving`'s walk positions, not cameras.
- **`Combat.Exit`** (`0x80023060`, after every frame, acting the frame a state asks, `0x800DB0A8`): marks the setup block used and clears `RememberParty` (`0x800D9474`); after the Goblin Ambush (978), however it ends, adds the party's first member's **base** Strength to its current and modified (`Stats.AddCurrent`: the ambush's second hit took it to 0, so a battle ended before that doubles it), sets flag 47, rewrites trek's kept place (`Zones.SavedPosition`) to Oriana's hut (map set 6, E03, (5.9, 0.5, 3.0), heading (1, 0), the eye at the position) and heals Alaron in full; takes every effect off every combatant (`Entity.ClearEffects`, `0x80071830`: the potions' but Stealth's, kind 0x10, and the 15 timed spells'; hit points stay); frees the GUI, the effects, the bodies, the struct and the zone engine; and last takes the dead out of the party (`Party.AfterBattle`, `0x80074EE8`, [Deaths](#deaths)). The bonus loot (`0x800D9470`) is never cleared, so a script's battle, which doesn't write it, pays out the last monster party's again. Where it goes is the mode the state returned:
  - **A win** (state 12, `Combat.StateVictory`, `0x80022450`), once faded out: `Combat.Exit` asked; for a monster party's battle alone (`RememberParty`, which only `BattleSetup.FromMonsterParty` sets), the encounter flag set, and if it still reads 0 (flag 0 or a derived flag) while the party's own gate holds (`MapObjects.GateHolds`, `0x800116AC`, over `0x800D9482` and `0x800D9484`), the party remembered (`MapObjectMemory.Remember`, kind 5, three map loads); `Combat.SetKillFlags` again; the snapshot, black; `PauseMenu.PrepareLoot` (`0x80048FF8`), which hands the loot window the loot and the gold and **adds the gold to the party at once**; the experience pool to `PauseMenu.ExperiencePool` (`0x800D94D0`); and mode 6, the post-battle windows: pause states 5 (experience and training) and 6 (loot) ([ui](ui.md#after-a-battle)). The loot window closes to trek (`PauseMenu.CloseToTrek`, `0x80048F40`), mode 1, built again at the kept place exactly and fading in at 1/30, as after fleeing. State 16 never runs for a win: the `Combat.NextState` 16 that `StateVictory` writes is never read.
  - **A loss** (state 13, `Combat.StateDefeat`, `0x80022590`), once faded out: `Combat.StartGameOverScript` (`0x8001F860`) heals the party's first member in full, finds the first dialogue object in the Dreamscape's map (Borg9 `0xD5F`) whose script is `CodeTable.GameOverScenes`' first, `0x0512` (the other two are never read), starts it as using it would, and sets state 21 (`Combat.StateLeaveToScript`, `0x800227D4`), which returns mode `0xC`, the cut-scene. The script's ending kind 2 (`CodeTable.ScriptEndings`, `0x800DFE1C`, kept in `CutScene.EndingKind` `0x800DFE4C`) makes the cut-scene return mode 0, the title (`Title.Frame`, `0x8001B154`): nothing is saved or reloaded. A non-lethal battle (`Combat.NonLethal`, `0x800DA0DC`, which `Combat.Init` sets for encounter flag 978 alone) instead sets the encounter flag, remembers the monster party fought with no gate check, sets the clock to 6:00:00 the next day whatever the hour (`World.SetDate`, `0x8007B914`, the day byte increased), and returns mode 1.
  - **Fleeing**: the pause menu's Flee, offered only in state 14 and while `0x800D9481` is set, with no confirmation, roll or line in the log (the combat strings' "The Party flees" is never read): it sets `Combat.NextState` to 20 (`pauseMenuSelectCallBack`, `0x800403AC`), and once the menu has rolled up, `Combat.StateLeaveFled` sets `Zones.Fled` (`0x800D9376`) and returns mode 1. `Zones.Fled` is read only by `InitZoneEngine` and cleared by `Zones.ResetBreadcrumbs`: fleeing costs nothing else, but the party isn't remembered and `Combat.Exit` takes the dead out as after any battle.
- **Back in trek.** As trek faded out, `TrekLoop.Free` kept where the player stood, his heading, the camera's eye and the chunk (`Zones.SavedPosition`, `0x800D906C`, 0x2C bytes). Trek is built again there (`InitGameTrek`, `0x80020F40`), the body put at the kept place exactly, not set down. Having fled, it's the **breadcrumb** farthest from there instead: trek's zone engine keeps where the player stands every 120 ticks, unless the nearest kept in the map set is under 10 away (the places within their chunks compared, as though in one), in a ring of 15 (`Zones.Breadcrumbs`, `0x800D90E0`; `HandleZoneEngineFrame`, `0x8000E784`), and `Zones.FindBreadcrumb` (`0x80009A48`) takes the farthest in the same map set, a chunk counting 100 a row or column, the last of equals. Every time trek is built, all 15 are where the player stands (`Zones.ResetBreadcrumbs`, `0x8000DF00`), so they aren't saved. Checked in checkpoints 01 and 02. The monster party that started the battle is remembered only on a win; after fleeing it's back at its home node with every wanderer, and can hunt again.
  - **The Shadow merging**: state 17, `Combat.MergeShadow` (`0x800675C0`), mode `0xE` (cinematic 4, [cinematics](cinematics.md)).

## The arenas

The Battle arenas are `aidyn:mapset/16` ("Battlefields", 150 to a cell): 28 chunks, the arena number being the chunk's place in the map set's list (`Zones.ArenaChunks`, `0x800DCA74`, which the importer reads), but only 15 maps. Chunks sharing a map are the same arena, terrain, scenes and music alike. `Zones.SetMapSet` puts the zone engine's point at (20, 20) in it.

- **What they place:** reference points and up to 12 scenes, all always there (flag 0 inverted), some only with the Expansion Pak. No lights, sounds or other objects. The 46 × 46 arenas are a flat disc of 24 triangles; the scenery reaches far past the collision, to ±300 in I11.
- **The points, by name** (`FindReferencePointName`, `0x8000B7C8`, with the combat strings' `%s%u%c`, `good`, `bad` and `flee%d`): every arena has the 40 spawns `good0a`–`good4d` and `bad0a`–`bad4d`, and `flee0` up to `flee7`. Arenas 17 and 20 have no flee points, so enemies there can't flee (`0x8005E76C` sets `+0x5440`).
- **Which spawn** (`Combat.FindSpawnPoint`, `0x8005C210`; `Combat.SpawnPosition`, `0x8005C294`): `%s%u%c`, the side's word, a set number and a letter. The party takes `good` and the enemies `bad`, swapped in an ambush (collision byte 2). Both sides start in set 4 − the animation byte, and reserves in set 0; in an ambush the party's set moves by the Ranger's roll (`Party.BattleAmbushRoll`, `0x80076C60`): the member, dead or not, with the strictly best modified Ranger who has the Stamina for it (5 less the Ranger, at least 1) rolls a d100 against Intelligence × 3 + Ranger × 10; failing, the set is 4 (3 with animation 3); under it, the margin's step (`Party.SkillCheckStep`) and the Stamina spent: step 1 moves it a set up (not with animation 0), 2 and 3 leave it, 4 or more a set down (not with animation 3); with no one to roll, no draw and no move. The party's letters are the living members' count so far, Sholeh's always `d`, and an enemy's its place in the list. A missing point stops the original (`FindReferencePointName` asserts). The spawn's x and z, truncated, are the combatant's cell (**checked**: Alaron's `bad2a` at (52.9, 52.3) is cell (52, 52)), and the body is made a unit up there and set down (mode 0).
- **Which way they face**: one draw each, as they're made: `Range(1, 3)` for the party, and for the enemies `Range(5, 7)` with collision byte 0, else `Range(1, 3)` too; the direction's vector is `Combat.DirectionVectors` (`0x800D27A8`, north (0, −1) round to north-west), and the body's heading is that way (the original's body field is its negation).
- **The ring's teardrops** are drawn by code, not placed (`CombatMarkers`, `combatmarkers.cpp`): a pool of 20 copies of `aidyn:scene/0x0794` (`CombatMarkers.Init`, `0x800854A8`, at `0x800E3138`, 0x1C each), a blended billboard about 0.41 × 1.0 with a scrolling texture (Borg1 1937); the code sets no scale, turn or scroll, and draws each with the zone's colours and fog (`Zones.SetSceneColors`). `CombatMarkers.Update` (`0x80085548`) works the radius out first, then lays the ring round where the combatant **began its turn** (`+0x08`), not where its body is: its radius R is the field's plus the body's scaled radius (`+0x34`), plus that again for one with an AI record; 6 teardrops up to R 3, 16 up to 7, else 20 (`CombatMarkers.CountFor`, `0x8008580C`, the floats at `0x800D43C8` and `0x800D43CC`); the first due +Z and the rest round toward −X by `(360 / N) × 0.017453`, the way round kept in two statics never reset (`0x800E3130`, `0x800E3134`, from (0, 1)), so each ring starts a ten-thousandth further round than the last. Each is set on the ground as a body is (`CombatMarkers.RingPoint`, `0x80085240`): a sphere of 0.5 swept from 100 to −100 past see-through triangles rests 0.01 above the first facing it; a miss tries again from a little to the side of the ring's point (two draws, up to 0.25 each way, not adding up), four times, and one that misses every time takes the body's height and then half way between its neighbours'. The ring is laid out only as a turn begins (and as the field follows a combatant near the Shadow), and drawn every frame (`CombatMarkers.Draw`, `0x80085848`, from `Combat.DrawFrame`) but from `Combatant.ExecuteAction` to the turn's end (`Combat.ActionActive`, `0x800DB0CC`) and from `Combat.StateVictory`; there's no fade in. **Checked** in 02, 03, 04, 09 and 10: every teardrop, where 03's sixteenth missed once and was tried again to the side.
- **The grid** (`ArenaGrid`, `0x800674A0`–`0x80068F7C`) is 100 × 100 cells of a byte a unit across in the arena chunk's own coordinates, index x × 100 + z, the height × 2 in the low bits and bit 7 for taken. `ArenaGrid.Init` (`0x80067960`) sizes it by the map's object grid (cell size × columns and rows, rounded half away from zero). `ArenaGrid.Fill` (`0x8005E900`) sweeps a sphere of 0.5 from 100 down to −100 through each cell's centre over the arena chunk's collision, see-through triangles included, the first facing it in the grid's order: the byte is twice the height it touches, truncated, and a miss is taken (0x80). Each combatant takes its footprint (`Occupy`, `0x8006820C`: from its cell on in +x and +z, none if any cell is outside), twice its scaled radius rounded half away from zero, at least 1 (the party 1, rats 2). **Checked**: the port's grid equals checkpoint 02's in all 8,330 cells. `WalkClear` (`0x80067E6C`) is a line over free cells with steps under 2; `HasSight` (`0x800684C4`) is the line in 3D for ranged attacks and spells, through the collision and the bodies between; `FindPath` (`0x80068CD4`) is a greedy walk of at most the grid's diagonal, which `StraightenPath` trims.

## The combat struct

`Combat.Current` (`0x800E0BF0`) points to 0x544C bytes (at `0x80282230` in the checkpoints; **checked** but where said):

| Offset | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `+0x00` | the part of the day as it began |
| `+0x01` | combatants in play (8, growing as reserves join, to 14) |
| `+0x02` | of them, active, able to act (8, then 7 after a rat died) |
| `+0x03`, `+0x04` | the party's size, and its members active |
| `+0x05`, `+0x06` | enemies on the field at the start, and enemies active |
| `+0x07` | the next reserve's place in the enemy list, `0xC` with none left (4, then 7 once three joined) |
| `+0x08` | u32, 1 after the first enemy died |
| `+0x0C` | u32, 1 once the leader's gone |
| `+0x10` | the leader: the first enemy with the highest base Intelligence, a later one needing strictly more |
| `+0x11` | the leader's morale: its record's at the start (`Combat.CreateEnemyCombatants`, `0x8005D048`), then as `EnemyAI.Morale` works it out in another enemy's check to flee ([The AI](#the-ai)) |
| `+0x12` | the AI's area danger, a radius the enemies spread out by (`EnemyAI.AreaDanger`, `0x80058A48`): set once in `Combat.Setup` to the party's flask tier (`Combat.PartyPotionTier`, `0x8005C750`: 7 holding an Inferno flask `0x1001`, else 5 a Fire flask `0x1000`, else 4 Sleep Gas `0x1002` or Acid `0x1003`, else 0), then raised as each living member is made (`Combat.CreatePartyCombatants`, `0x8005C7C8`, and `CreateSholeh`, `0x8005CA60`) to twice its Fireball's casting level, `min(known, modified Wizard)`; nothing changes it during the battle |
| `+0x13` | the Troubadour's bonus in play, 0 with none |
| `+0x14` | the last target |
| `+0x18`, `+0x1C` | f32, the camera's focus: the combatant's, or its target's |
| `+0x20`, `+0x24` | u32, the gold and experience pools (0 and 1000 for the rats) |
| `+0x28` | the enemy list (the setup block's) |
| `+0x2C` | the current combatant |
| `+0x30` | the turn order: u8 turns in the round, u8 the one now, u32 `+0x34` the alternation toggle, `+0x38` the list of combatant indexes |
| `+0x3C` | the arena grid, 0x4E28 bytes: two 100 × 100 layers (heights and occupied; path marks), `+0x4E20` u16 area, `+0x4E22` and `+0x4E23` width and depth (85 × 98 for the rats'), `+0x4E24` and `+0x4E25` their halves, `+0x4E26` the diagonal |
| `+0x4E64` | target set A, for attacks |
| `+0x4F0C` | target set B, for spells |
| `+0x4FB4` | the loot, an inventory made at victory |
| `+0x4FB8` | a message popup |

## A turn

1. **It ends** (`Combatant.EndTurn`, `0x80061300`): `CombatCamera.Reset` (`0x8008D210`, a glide stopped), the AI's target forgotten (`EnemyAI.ClearTarget`, `0x8005B998`), the cast chosen cancelled (the entity's `+0x3C` to `0xFF`; the selected spell stays), the acted flag cleared, a scroll or wand whose selected spell has no charges left taken off and gone (the entity's `+0x3D` of 5 with an item of type `0x11` or `0x0D`, `Entity.UnequipGear` `0x8006F5C4`) and no spell selected, state 1 and the ring shown again (`Combat.ActionActive` 0), the body asked to stand, the action put back (`Combatant.RevertAction`, `0x8006679C`) and with a flask in hand the weapon stowed (`Party.StowHeldItem`, `0x800763B0`), the last target forgotten if it's dead, then `TurnOrder.Advance`. Every end of a turn comes here: the skip, the action's result (state 4, 60 ticks on), the AI's rest, a weapon or flask taken in hand in the pause menu (19), Sense Aura's popup dismissed, and the AI's walk or turn with nothing to do (5, 6).
2. **The next** (`TurnOrder.Advance`, `0x8006AC4C`): nothing once the battle's won or lost (states 12, 13); the party's first member dead (`*GameState.Party`, not `Combat.AlaronSlot`) loses it (`Combat.Defeat`, `0x80023F98`: state 13 and the fade, or 28 in a non-lethal battle); the last combatant's body let go of its field (`TurnOrder.StopBody`, `0x8006A5C4`); then on through the round, its end building the next (step 7). Before each candidate the battle's end is checked (`TurnOrder.CheckEnd`, `0x8006A97C`, over `TurnOrder.CountActive`, `0x8006A880`, a reserve still to come counting as an enemy), and the candidate's radius, place and whether it can act are worked out (`Combatant.UpdateActive`, `0x8006615C`: not with no Intelligence, Willpower, Dexterity or Strength, nor immobilised), skipping it dead, unable or gone. Then the focus is on it, every combatant reads its body (`TurnOrder.ClearActions`, `0x8006AE64`, clearing the numbers to show), its turn begins, the clock moves on 600 (`World.AdvanceTurn`, `0x8007C01C`: `World.WrapYear` and `World.Update`, so the weather rolls if the part of the day changed; it ages no effects; **checked**, +600 a turn in 02–04), and a reserve that fled on arriving says "Enemy reinforcements flee" (`+0x543C`).
3. **It begins** (`TurnOrder.BeginTurn`, `0x8006A9C8`): the camera's given to its body (`GiveCameraToPlayer`, `0x80015E30`, which drops an R orbit) and anchored there; its line, "%s begins action", a newcomer's "joins the battle" (`0x7C`, clearing flag 10), or either overwritten by a performer's (`0xD0`); it reads its body (`Combatant.SyncFromBody`) and keeps where it starts (`SaveStartPosition`); the ring (which works the radius out again); its field (`Combatant.PlaceRingAndBody`, `0x8006627C`); then for a combatant with an AI record, its check to flee (`EnemyAI.CheckFlee`, `0x800572AC`: the leader's morale and its own against `RollD(2, 6)`) and state 8, otherwise a flask or spell carried over looked up and state 1, as `State` and `NextState` both, after the target sets are built. The Shadow's aura is the round's, not the turn's, and nothing sets a body down: bodies are set down once, as they're made.
4. **The field's radius** (`+0x21`, `Combatant.UpdateRadius`, `0x8005F29C`) is the first place in `Combat.RadiusByDexterity` (`0x800E0C20`: 0, 0, 0, 3, 7, 12, 16, 21, 26, 28, 30, 33, 36, 38) whose byte reaches the **modified** Dexterity, 14 past it (3 for 1–3, 4 for 4–7, … 13 for 37–38). Then each timed effect in its slot's order: Haste (kind `0x1E`) adds its level, and Stellar Gravity (`0x38`) takes its level away, leaving 1 if that's all or more; an effect's kind is its spell ID's low byte's place in the code's spell list (`0x800E2B40`). Then **1 at most while engaged**: an opponent alive and able within the larger of each body's radii, scaled (`+0x34`) or not (`+0x38`), plus 0.75 (`0x800D2810`), across the ground, both bodies read again first (`Combatant.IsWithinReach`, `0x8006011C`, from `IsFreeOfOpponents`, `0x8005F1B8`); this sets or clears flag 7 and stops a performance. Not engaged, 1 at most while performing, or 1 near the Shadow (`Combat.NearShadow`, `0x80023F34`: within 10, strictly, which logs the vortex line on the current one's). Then 13 at most, and 0 for Pochanargat (entity list place `0xAB`, `0x800E28EB`). It's worked out as each body is made, before each candidate in `Advance`, with each ring, and in the Shadow's battle as the combatant walks, so flag 7 is as old as that: **checked** in 09, where Brenna is engaged with a rat whose own flag is clear until its turn (10). Every radius in 02, 03, 04, 09 and 10 matches.
5. **The player** moves the body with trek's stick handler (`Body.ApplyInput`, `0x80014FEC`, fed each poll): R orbits the camera as in trek, and otherwise up on the stick is the way the camera looks, walking, running from 0.8 or sneaking with Z held, a party member turning 10° a poll. The field holds it: `Body.SetGoal` (`0x80015BD4`) keeps a centre (the combatant's place and the body's height as its turn began, `+0x274`) and a radius (`+0x280`) with body flag 4, and `ProcessPlayers` (`Body.ClampToGoal`, `0x80014518`) puts the body's X and Z back on that **sphere** after it moves, before the bodies push each other, whenever it's at the radius or past it in 3D. So it slides round the edge, and on a slope reaches a little less far across the ground. **Checked**: 03's walked bodies are 10.0000 from their centres in 3D. Every body gets its field as it's made, about where it stands, and loses it as its turn ends. Nothing in the grid stops a body; the bodies' push does (`Crowd`). After each poll the combatant reads its body (`Combatant.UpdateFromBody`, `0x8005F784`): its footprint leaves the grid and takes it again where the body stands (`ArenaGrid.Vacate`, `0x800682E0`, and `Occupy`, the cell rounded half away from zero), its place is the body's X and Z exactly, the focus follows and its facing is the body's; a change counts a move (`+0x22`), the fourth sets flag 3 (**checked** in 02–04), and the target sets are built again. The radius isn't worked out again as it walks up to an enemy: `Combat.MovePlayerBody` (`0x80084CE0`) does that only near the Shadow, centring the field on the body.
6. **The action** ends the turn: an attack, melee or ranged by the weapon (`Combatant.ResolveAction`, `0x80065ACC`); a flask thrown or a potion drunk (action 3: `ThrowFlask` `0x80065600`, `DrinkPotion` `0x80064C84`); a spell (`CastCurrentSpell`, `0x80065C08`); Lay On Hands or Heal With Herbs (`ResolveHealer`, `0x8006591C`); the Troubadour (`StartTroubadour`, `0x800662F4`); a weapon changed in the pause menu; or a skip. `Combatant.ExecuteAction` (`0x80065F6C`) ends a performance, hides the ring, carries it out, applies the damage, checks for death (`CheckDeath`, `0x80065E98`) and starts state 2.
7. **The round ends** (`TurnOrder.EndRound`, `0x8006A62C`) when the list runs out: the Shadow's aura first (`Combat.ShadowAura`, `0x80023AD0`, which can end the battle); each performer pays a Stamina point or stops with none; every combatant's moves and flag 3 are cleared and its effects aged a round (`Character.AgeEffects`, `0x80071444`: the potions', the timed spells', and what they do); the reserves come in (`Combat.SpawnReserves`); the toggle flips and the next list is built. The leader's morale moves at each of the AI's turns, not here. Wins and losses are only ever found at a turn's change, not at a death.

**Turn order** (`TurnOrder.Build`, `0x8006A16C`). A combatant's turns a round are by its **base** Dexterity (`Combatant.TurnsPerRound`, `0x8005F4D0`): 1–17 one, 18–24 one and two by turns (the toggle), 25–29 two, 30–34 two and three by turns, 35 and up three. The list is grouped: every third turn first, then every second, then every first, each group in random order (`Rng.Range`, drawing again on a repeat). Checked in 02: four party members and four rats gave `[1,3,0,2, 3,2,1,5,4,7,0,6]`.

**Win and loss** (`TurnOrder.CheckEnd`, `0x8006A97C`): no party member active is a loss (`TurnOrder.Defeat`, `0x8006A780`: state 13 and `Combat.Defeat`, then with a current combatant the party's line, `0xD4`, and in a non-lethal battle Alaron's own over it for good, `0x74`, his portrait locked), and no enemy active with no reserve left a win (`TurnOrder.Victory`, `0x8006A804`: `Combat.SetKillFlags` `0x80067860`, `Combat.TallyRewards` `0x8005E2AC`, state 12, the fade out at the battle's 1/60, and with a current combatant the victory line but under encounter flag 978). Alaron's death is a loss alone (`TurnOrder.Advance`, straight to `Combat.Defeat`, with no line). `Combat.Defeat` (`0x80023F98`) is state 13 and the fade out (`Combat.EnterDefeat`, `0x80023FD0`), or in a non-lethal battle state 28 (`Combat.EnterDefeatWait`, `0x80024014`).

**The kill flags** (`Combat.SetKillFlags`) read the enemy list's first four entries, stopping at the first empty, and set the flag of each boss among them (a jump table at `0x800D2B60`, by entity list place from `0xA8`): Kitarak 154, Pochanargat 3204, Shamsuk 458, Ksathra 3738, Shatrevar 3737, Mehrdad 3736, Golnar 3735, Nasim 3734, Behrooz 3733, Assim 3739 and Lugash 533; the Lizard Man Boss, the Marquis, the Shadow, Sheridan and four more set none. **Rewards** (`Combat.TallyRewards`), as the battle is won, before the fade: the loot inventory (`+0x4FB4`, 64 stacks) and the pools are made; for **every** entry of the enemy list, killed, fled or never come alike, its experience (the entity's `+0x7C`, the DB's reward byte × 50: a rat 100) and its chest (the entity's `+0x7E`, the chest DB's ID `0x7FF` + the byte, through `Combat.AddChestLoot` `0x8005E0F0`); then the bonus loot (`Combat.AddBonusLoot`, `0x8005E238`: a chest if its type is 8, rolled with no entity, else one of the item, worth no experience); then each living member gains the whole experience pool (`Character.AddExp`, `0x80071528`, times 1.5: dead members gain nothing, and an ally in the enemy list gives nothing); and last the gold pool is **doubled** (`sll` at `0x8005E400`). A chest rolls, in this order, on the one stream: its gold (`Range` between its bounds, drawing nothing when they're equal); the enemy's own armor and shield, each by the chest's chance (`RollD(1, 100)`) if it has them, and its three weapons, each only of a class that drops (`Combat.DropWeaponClasses`, `0x800D26C8`: Hafted, Missile, Pole, Sword and Thrown) (`Combat.RollEquipmentDrops`, `0x8005DC78`); a reagent by its chance, then which (`Range(30, 32)`: spice, herb or gemstone) and how many; then the six drops (`Combat.RollChestItem`, `0x8005DF38`), the first two between their counts. **What's looted adds to the experience pool**: a weapon its `ExpModifier`, armor its `ExpBonus`, another item its `ExpMultiplier` (the reagents 5, the Sapphire Gem 500), a potion nothing; a reagent once whatever the count, a drop times its count. **Checked**: the original's `TallyRewards`, run on checkpoint 11's RAM, gives 12's pool of 1000 and every member's experience, +1500 each, and the port's matches it for eight lists and seeds, gold, loot and experience alike (`VictoryCheckpointTests`). ## An attack

Read from 1.0's code, checked by running the original's own functions on the checkpoints' RAM, and replayed by the port (`AttackCheckpointTests`).

**Target set A** (`+0x4E64`; `TargetSet.Rebuild`, `0x80069264`) is built at a turn's start, as the combatant moves (`UpdateFromBody`), as an aim is put away and by other callers; while a player aims (states 9, 10, 18) it isn't rebuilt on idle polls. `Rebuild` empties it, finds the candidates (`FindAttackTargets`, `0x80069304`: everyone in play `Combatant.CanAttack`, `0x80060590`, allows; one may be chosen), selects the first, and keeps the cursor on the last target (`+0x10`), else where it was (`RestoreCursor`, `0x8006A05C`). Without an action, and not healing, it stays empty.

- **`CanAttack`**: never the Shadow (entity `0x0226`); not healing, a target on the other side, not dead or gone, while the attacker has an action; its weapon reaching (`CanReachWithWeapon`): a flask always; melee and healing within reach (`IsWithinReach`, the bodies' radii plus 0.75); ranged from distance 3 to the weapon's range, truncated, with no opponent within reach; melee-or-ranged anywhere in range. Then **`ArenaGrid.WalkClear`** (`0x80067E6C`) over the grid between the two cells, or failing that **`ArenaGrid.HasSight`** (`0x800684C4`) from the attacker's body lifted by both heads' heights to the target's body's centre: the two lifts (the entity's height less the body's radius) are worked out for each, but the second block, pasted from the first with only its registers changed, adds the target's to the attacker's point too (`0x8006079C` and `0x800607DC`, both to `sp+0x24`; the target's vector at `sp+0x60` is never lifted), so the line comes steeply down to the target's middle and a body just before the target blocks it. `CanCast` lifts the target's own point. The port runs it head to head (below). The line is a sphere of 0.1 swept through the collision, see-through triangles included, and no other living body within its unscaled radius of the line, which runs on past both ends.
- **`WalkClear`** steps the line's longer axis cell by cell: each must be inside, not taken unless the end is a step from it, and a step from the last unless the end isn't. A step is a rise under 2 in the grid's half units (`IsStep`, `HeightDifference`). But it steps only toward +X or +Z: going the other way its loop never starts and the line is clear unchecked, so a target with a smaller X (or Z, for a line longer in Z) never needs the 3D test.
- **The action** (`+0x25`, `Combatant.SetDefaultAction`, `0x800672D4`): no weapon, none, so **unarmed party members can't attack**; range 0, melee; else ranged, or melee-or-ranged for a Breath, a Spit or Spikes (`Weapon.AllowsMelee`, `0x8007B730`). Enemies have weapons too: a rat's is Rat Bite (`aidyn:weapon/0x0702`).

**Aiming** (state 1's A, `Combat.InputTurn`, with the stick centred and R let go): set A is built again, and with candidates `TargetSet.BeginAttack` (`0x800694A8`) orders them (`OrderByAngle`, `0x8006975C`) and puts the cursor on the last target, else where it was, else the first; state 9, the camera given to the cursor's body. A bow, a thrown weapon that flies or a flask (`+0x8A`, `Combatant.UpdateDrawnFlag`, `0x80066A2C`, through the bow list `0x800E0C08` and the thrown list `0x800E0C14`) takes state 18 instead, a free cursor at the target. The order is by where each body stands from the attacker's against its body's own heading field, the opposite of where it looks: ahead and to one side by the sine's size, descending; then behind and the other side, ascending; then ahead and the other side, ascending; then behind and the one side, descending (`Vec2.Cosine`, `Vec2.SignedSine`, a selection sort whose ties go to the later). It sets only the cycling order and the cursor's place.

**State 9** (`Combat.InputAttackTarget`, `0x800842C4`), each poll, edge-triggered, with no repeat:

- **Start** pauses, and the pause comes back to state 1, the camera to the combatant.
- **B, Z or L** put it away (`Combat.ReturnToTurn`, `0x80083C70`): state 1, the healing modes off, the focus on the combatant, both sets built again, the camera back. The body stays turned.
- **A** confirms (`ConfirmAttackTarget`, below).
- **The stick or the D-pad**, left, steps the cursor +1 through the order, and right −1 (`0x80084420`), the camera to the new target and the attacker's body turning toward it 10° a frame (`Combat.AimAtCursor`, `0x80084334`).
- **The camera**: with the cursor's body over 5 from the attacker's, the eye's goal is 4 behind the attacker, away from it, and 1.5 up (`CombatCamera.PullBack`, `0x8008D22C`), with `Camera::Mode1`'s last flag clear, which skips its rise, its leash and its rescue.

**State 18** (`Combat.InputThrownTarget`, `0x80084BA0`): C-left and C-right step the candidates; the stick moves a free cursor 0.8 a frame, turned by the camera and kept on the grid and within the weapon's range, and set A selects the candidate whose body is nearest it, within a unit (`0x80084A24`); A confirms the same way.

**The turn to face** (`ConfirmAttackTarget`, `0x80084078`): the target's slot goes in `+0x14`, set B is emptied and A keeps its selection but loses its candidates, the camera goes back to the attacker, its facing points at the target and its body's turn starts (`Combatant.FaceCombatant`, `0x8005FD28`), state 6. State 6's input (`Combat.InputFacing`, `0x80085018`) snaps the heading to the goal on every poll while the body's turning flag (`0x20`) is set, so the 10° turn never plays: **state 6 takes two frames whatever the angle**, then `Combat.StateFacing` (`0x800221B0`) carries out the attack.

**`Combatant.ExecuteAction`** (`0x80065F6C`), in order: a performance ends; the target is the last (`+0x10`); set B is emptied; the attack is resolved (`ResolveAction`, `0x80065ACC`: melee, ranged, or for melee-or-ranged melee if `CanAttack` holds as melee; then the attacker's request by its weapon's animation byte, `Combat.AnimationByWeaponClass` `0x800D27E0`: 0 → 16, 1 → 17, 2 → 13, 3 → 12, 4 → 15, 5 → 14, else 18); the Goblin Ambush's hold on Alaron (`Combat.ShadowFightTrick`, `0x80065DCC`); **`CheckDeath`** (`0x80065E98`) at once, before any hit point goes: a blow that takes them queues "%s killed" (`0x50`) and calls **`Combatant.Death`** now, or with a Cheat Death on the target queues "%s cheats death" (`0xFC`); the attacker's moves set to 1; **the damage stored on the target** (`+0x29`, a byte); the camera's glide from the attacker to the target (`CombatCamera.StartGlide`); and state 2 with `ActionActive`. The acted flag stays down (only `PrepareCast` sets it), so a weapon's spell rolls its own margin.

**Melee** (`Combatant.MeleeChance`, `0x800622C4`, every step truncated to 16 bits):

- The attacker's modified Strength and Endurance, base Level, the weapon's hit, 6 × its weapon skill (the modified one, `Skills.WeaponModified`) and 2 × Warrior.
- Less the target's 2 × modified Dexterity, base Level and shield defence (`Combatant.ShieldDefence`, `0x8005F6B0`: the shield's defence × the shield skill + Warrior, a byte, so Warrior counts without a shield). The Dexterity and shield don't count against a target held fast (`IsImmobilised`).
- 20 more from behind, and Thief × 10 (`SkillTerm`, `0x80062ED8`).
- × 1.15 (`0x800D2964`) against a target with no Stamina; the height, c + h × 0.05 × c, with h the attacker's cell's height byte less the target's (`0x7F` off the grid); × 0.8 with no Stamina of its own; × 0.9 at night; the aspect (`AspectScale`: a Lunar attacker × 1.25 at night and × 0.75 by day, a Solar one the other way round, `0x800D2990`); the Troubadour (`TroubadourScale`); at least 5.
- **From behind** (`Combatant.IsBehind`, `0x8005FB54`): the point the distance away along the attacker's facing lies within half a unit of the target in X and Z (`IsAimedAt`, `0x8005FA44`), and the two facings' dot product, rounded half away from zero to six places, is at least 0.7071.

**`ResolveMelee`** (`0x800628A4`): a d100, which `AdjustRoll` (`0x80062594`, magic's) turns with Warrior: at or under min(Warrior, a fifth of the chance), a second d100 comes off it. A hit is the adjusted roll under the chance.

- **A hit** draws the damage, then a d100 for the weapon skill (`Combatant.GainSkillExperience`, `0x80062650`: at or under a tenth of (8 − skill)² for a skill under 6, else 5, the base trains a level; half the points on a miss). It shows and queues "%s attacks %s" (`0x24`) or, the roll adjusted, "%s scores a critical hit on %s" (`0x2C`), and queues "%s loses %d hit points" (`0x30`; `0x34` for 1).
- **A miss** draws the experience, shows "attacks" and queues "%s avoids the attack" (`0x20`).
- So a melee hit draws the roll, [the adjustment], the dice, [the weapon's spell], the experience. **A critical is only its line**: the second d100's bigger margin is its only effect.

**`MeleeDamage`** (`0x80062AE4`): (the weapon's dice + the Strength tier) d6 (`Combatant.StrengthTier`, `0x80062008`: the first of 4, 9, 17, 24, 28, 32, 36, 39 at `0x800E0C30` the modified Strength is at or under, else 8); plus the margin's step (`SkillCheckStep`, `0x800DA348`) and the attacker's base Level; less protection (`ProtectionVsAttack`, `0x8005F71C`: `TotalProtection`, `0x8005F570`, the sheet's, body armor's and a fifth of Warrior, 0 if under 1, plus the shield's unless from behind, a byte); plus Thief × 2 from behind; at least 1. Then **`ApplyResistance`** (`0x80062704`): the product of every resistance of the target's own and its gear's that meets the weapon's element (`ResistanceApplies`, `0x80061A30`: the element, Physical also Cutting and Smashing, Magic every magical one), truncated, at least 1 unless one is 0; a weakness over 1 makes an enemy's morale check due. Then the weapon's spell's damage (`Combatant.WeaponSpellProc`, `0x800627D0`), cast as any spell, so its roll's step is kept as the attacker's cast margin (`+0x3C`, `PrepareCast`'s `0x80064184`) until the turn ends; nothing reads it. The sheet's own damage byte (`CharExp+3`) and the entity's damage aren't read.

**Ranged** (`Combat.RangedChance`, `0x80062F50`): the attacker's modified Intelligence and Dexterity, base Level, the hit, 6 × the skill and 2 × Warrior; less (distance − 5) past 6; less the target's base Level, and unless it's held, its 2 × Dexterity and shield defence; 20 from behind (no Thief); × 1.2 against a target that hasn't moved or acted (`+0x22` 0), × 1.15 against one with no Stamina, × (h × 0.05 + 1), × 0.9 with no Stamina of its own, × (1 − the rain's intensity × 0.3), × 0.7 at night, × 0.8 against one that has moved; the aspect, the Troubadour, at least 5. `ResolveRanged` (`0x80063324`) is `ResolveMelee` with no Strength dice and no blow from behind in the damage (so the shield's protection always counts), the experience after the lines, and "%s misses %s" (`0x38`). No ammunition is spent. `Combat.WeatherPenalty` (`0x80062D78`) is `ThrowChance`'s, not this; `Combatant.HeightModifier` (`0x80062E0C`) has no caller.

**The action's states** (ticks are the frame's delta; the action timer is `+0x52CC`):

- **State 2** (`Combat.StateAction`, `0x80021774`) waits for the swing (`Combat.StepActionTimer`, `0x80021708`: the attacker's body's `+0x18` back to 0, and over 29 ticks). Then an arrow or thrown weapon flies (below), or the blow lands: with damage, `Combat.ApplyHit` (`0x80021930`) takes it through `Character.TakeDamage`, sets the target's request 7 and shows the number over its head (`Combat.ShowDamage`, `0x80021294`); with none, an opponent dodges (request 8) and an ally is helped (10, with healing's number); a dead target falls (`PlayDeathAnimation`, request 9 at once). Then state 4, with the queued line shown (`FlushQueuedLog`). So **the numbers come after the swing**: 111 ticks for Alaron's sword (request 12).
- **State 4** (`Combat.StateResult`, `0x80021F30`): `Combat.RemoveFallen` (`0x80021D2C`) waits for the target's body's `+0x18` to be 0 and 60 ticks (a rat's fall is 239), then frees a dead one's body (`Combat.FreeBody`, `0x80021C6C`: its effects and potions off, its numbers gone, its portrait skulled, `Combat.Bodies[slot]` emptied, the record kept), gives the camera back to the attacker and deselects the target. With no target left, the turn ends.
- **The one-shot requests**: after every frame `Combat.AfterFrame` (`0x80023360`) zeroes each body's request but 0–3, `0x14`–`0x16` and `0x19`, and `ProcessPlayers` copies the request to `+0x18` whenever the actor enters a state, so `+0x18` goes back to 0 when the swing or the reaction ends and the actor idles. A dead body (`+0x18` 9, its request no longer 9) holds its last frame (`+0x77C`), where `+0x18` takes 0.

**`Combatant.Death`** (`0x80062080`): the immobilising effects come off, its footprint leaves the grid, the active counts fall (`+0x02`, and `+0x04` or `+0x06`), Sholeh's death sets flag 622, a party member stops performing, and the first enemy's death sets `+0x08`. It doesn't set flag 6 or free the body: a dead combatant is known by its hit points. A death from the effects at a round's end (`Entity.ShowAgeResult`, `0x8007138C`) shows the number, then `Death` and `FreeBody` at once: no fall, no line, no wait.

**Arrows and thrown weapons** fly as the spells' cast scene (`Combat.CastScene`, `0x800DB0C8`, state 3). After the swing, `Combat.StartCastCamera` loads the arrow (Borg5 `0x348F`) for a bow, else the weapon's own model (`Combatant.LoadWeaponScene`, `0x80066CF8`), from the hand's locator to the target's middle (its feet plus half its height). It goes in a straight line in X and Z, `v` a tick, raised by sin(travelled / L × π) × `arc`, with `f = muzzle + (L ≤ 40 ? L/40 × 4 − 2 : 2)`, `v = f > 10 ? 0.5 : f ≥ 1 ? f × 0.05 : 0.05`, `a = 10 − muzzle + (L ≤ 40 ? L/40 × 8 − 4 : 4)` and `arc = a > 10 ? 7 : a ≥ 1 ? a × 0.7 : 0.7`, the muzzle number by weapon (`Combatant.WeaponMuzzle`, `0x80066DD0`, the combat record's `missiles`). It's scaled as the attacker and not turned along its way. The blow lands 30 ticks after it arrives (`Combat.EndCast`, `0x800219D8`). **`CombatAttackFx.Add` (`0x80089B80`) has no caller** in either build.

**The numbers over heads** (`CombatStatIndicator`, `0x8008A000`–`0x8008A8D4`): a pool of 16. Each is its number (a byte, 1–3 digits) put into a 64 × 16 texture from the digit textures (Borg1 22, 17, 21, 20, 15, 14, 19, 18, 13, 16 for 0–9, upside down), centred, at the body's X and Z and its head's height (half a unit higher for healing), and stays there. It's a billboard of 0.5 × 0.2 (healing 1.5 × 0.6), growing by s × scale across and s × 0.4 × scale up and rising s × 0.5 × scale, with s = sin(t / 120 × 90°), negative for healing, and scale its distance from the camera × 0.15; coloured (255, 255, 128) for damage and (128, 128, 255) for healing, its alpha `(u8)(base − (s8)(s × 255))` from 255 (0 for healing); gone at 120 ticks. Nothing is shown for a miss, a critical or a death.

**The target marks** (`CombatTargetMarks.Draw`, `0x80089830`) aren't a cursor. Every frame, in every state, each combatant selected in set A (set B in states 10 and 11) has its body's light colour go toward a grey of |sin(n × 3°)|, n its ticks marked (`Body.SetTint`, `0x8001371C`): a pulse from dark to full once a second, ending at once when it's deselected (`Body.ClearTint`, `0x80013774`). So the selected enemy pulses in state 1 as soon as one is in reach.

**Reserves** (`Combat.SpawnReserves`, `0x8005D914`, at a round's end after the effects age; running it on 08 gives 09's cells). It refills **every** enemy slot whose combatant is dead or gone, or that's empty, from the party's size to `+0x01`, while the list has a next (`+0x07`):

- A reserve that flees as it arrives (`EnemyAI.ReserveFleesOnArrival`, session 6) is marked and skipped, its slot left empty, with the hold's line.
- The leader's slot taken sets `+0x0C`.
- The newcomer stands at `Combat.ChooseSpawn` (`0x8005D748`, through `SpawnPosition`: one draw for its direction, `Range(5, 7)` with collision byte 0, else `Range(1, 3)`; set 0, the enemies' side, the slot's letter, `a` + slot − party size), or if that cell is taken, or a body's centre is within its radius and the newcomer's, the first free and clear point of sets 0–4 in turn, its side's letters first (`0x8005D5B0`, `0x8005D474`).
- Its body, radius, field, portrait and action are made as `Combat.CreateBody` makes them, with flag `0x400`, and `+0x07` moves on. The counts aren't changed: `CountActive` finds the newcomers at the next turn's change. Checkpoint 09's newcomers walking is their own AI turn, not the spawn's.

**The random stream between checkpoints**: besides the battle's own draws, `Weather.DriftWind` (`0x8007BAA0`, from `Combat.AfterFrame` through `World.SetTerrain` and `World.Update`) draws 2 every drawn frame, plus 3 with chance 0.05 and 1 more with 0.05. It accounts for every draw from 04 to 05 (1,907 in 873 frames). The attack's own draws between 05 and 06 can be counted but not placed from two states.

## The AI

Read from 1.0's code (`0x80056E10`–`0x8005C0C0`, `aiInfo.cpp` and `aiscores.cpp`), checked by running the original's own functions on the checkpoints' RAM in session 5's MIPS interpreter, and replayed by the port (`AiCheckpointTests`, `EnemyAiTests`).

**The record** (0x1C, `EnemyAI.Init`, `0x8005BA7C`; `EnemyAI.Free`, `0x8005BD90`):

| Offset | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `+0x00` | the spare weapon: an item made from the entity's first weapon, only when its first and third are both set; what `EnemyAI.SwapWeapon` trades the held one for |
| `+0x04` | the type it fights as: 0 melee, 1 ranged, 2 caster, from the weapon and spells; `ChooseWeapon` makes it `+0x07` |
| `+0x05` | the target's slot, `0xFF` none |
| `+0x06` | morale, the entity record's (`+0x1B` in RAM): 5 for rats, 12 and up never flee; spells of kinds `0x19` and `0x2F` lower it until the effect ends, when `EffectRules.End` (`0x800707EC`) gives back what they took ([magic](magic.md)) |
| `+0x07` | the type its style wants this turn; unlike `+0x04`, `Execute` swaps weapons first |
| `+0x08` | the attack style, 0–10 (`EnemyAI.StyleTable`, `0x800E0B80`, a function every 8 bytes) |
| `+0x09` | flags: 1 fleeing, 2 a morale check due, 4 two more morale (nothing sets it), 8 the AI plays its turns (set by `Init`, tested by `TurnOrder.BeginTurn`), `0x10` hit by a weakness (never cleared) |
| `+0x0A`–`+0x11` | eight action slots, spell kinds or `0xFF`: the entity's first five spells in its record's order (its spellbook's places 0–4), its armor's, its shield's and its weapon's own spell (the one it's cast from, not the one it strikes with); a slot chosen becomes the entity's selected spell by `EnemyAI.SlotIndex` and `SlotKind` (`0x800E0B70`, `0x800E0B78`: source 1 with the place for 0–4, then 2, 3 and 4). No enemy has an armor's or shield's spell; Kitarak, Shamsuk, Lugash and Golnar have a weapon's |
| `+0x12`, `+0x13` | the path's next corner and its length |
| `+0x14` | the combatant |
| `+0x18` | a flight's turns left: 4 as it starts |

**The style** (`Init`): no weapon, 5; a melee weapon, 0, or 1 with a base Intelligence of 15 or more, or with any spell 6 (9 for the bosses, `EnemyAI.HasBossStyle`, `0x8005BA40`, a jump table at `0x800D24B0`: entity list places `0x57`–`0x59`, `0x5E`, `0x5F`, `0x7A`, `0xA7`–`0xA9`, `0xAB`, `0xAD`, `0xBA`, `0xBC`, the combat record's `BossStyles`); a ranged one, 2, or 3 clever, 4 if it strikes in melee too (`Weapon.AllowsMelee`), or with a spell 7, 8 (10 for the bosses). Of the 114 entities that fight: style 0 32 (rats, wolves, trolls, skeletons, Goblin `0x0215`), 1 7, 2 7 (Goblin `0x022E`, Goblin Scout `0x024E`), 3 12 (Goblin Scout `0x0208`, Bandit Woodsmen, the Goblin Poisoners), 4 3, 5 6, 6 28, 7 2, 8 4, 9 7, 10 6.

**An enemy's weapon** (the entity's build in its enemy mode, `0x8006E2E8` through `0x8006E434`): the third weapon slot's if it can wield it, else the first's; the second is never read. Every entity with a third weapon can, so the first is the fallback only for those without. The goblin archers hold a bow with a sword as the spare.

**A turn** (`EnemyAI.Step`, `0x8005B314`, the first frame of state 8). An enemy decides once and walks once: one plan, at most one leg, then at most one action or a rest.

1. It reads its body (`Combatant.SyncFromBody`) and keeps where it starts.
2. Not fleeing, its style chooses (`EnemyAI.ChooseGoal`, `0x8005B144`; `EnemyAI.RunStyle`, `0x80059628`): the style fills `AiScores` and sorts it, and the first entry is the target (`+0x05`) and the goal cell. At the goal already, it acts (`Execute`). Fleeing, the goal is the nearest flee point (below).
3. **The path** (`EnemyAI.PlanPath`, `0x8005A974`; `ArenaGrid.FindPath`, `0x80068CD4`): both footprints are freed; from the start it steps to the free, unmarked, gentle neighbour (a rise under 3 half units, `ArenaGrid.IsGentle`) nearest the goal by the distance squared, the first of equals in the directions' order N to NW, marking each, until the goal or the grid's diagonal. It never searches: at a dead end it counts back without stepping back, so any pocket, a ring of bodies or a wall's corner between it and the goal, fails it. The grid is filled again with every living body that's still there (`ArenaGrid.RefillWithBodies`), and a path is cut to its corners (`TrimPath`, `0x80068784`, takes the loops out; `StraightenPath`, `0x8006894C`, keeps a run's last cell). With no path: walk straight at the target if it's within its bodies' radii and 2.25 (`EnemyAI.ApproachTarget`, `0x8005AB14`), else act from where it stands.
4. **The leg** (`EnemyAI.WalkPath`, `0x8005A0D8`): corner after corner, each queued for the body to run to (`SetPlayerMoveToQueue`, `0x800157C8`, a ring of 16) and the facing that way, until the path's end or a stop. A corner that's taken or too steep stops it at the first blocked cell of the straight line there (`EnemyAI.WalkIsClear`, `0x80059C94`, a Bresenham line), so a rat runs into its target's cell and the bodies' push holds it off. A corner past the field, measured from the turn's start, stops it where the line leaves the circle (`EnemyAI.ClipToCircle`, `0x80059A84`, whose quadratic has b where 2b belongs), rounded: an engaged enemy, of radius 1, moves a cell. The combatant stands on the leg's end at once, on the grid too, while its body runs; flag 3 is set for a leg of 3 or more. State 5, the stuck timer at 240. If no corner could be walked it only turns toward the first (`EnemyAI.FaceStep`) and approaches or acts.
5. **The walk** (state 5): the body runs (stick 0.95, `Body.WalkStick`) to each point in turn, arriving within its scaled radius. A body that comes no nearer by 0.01 for 30 ticks, counted over the whole point, gives it up where it stands in combat (`Body.StepTowardGoal`, `0x800159C8`, which outside combat puts it at the goal); and every tick it moves under 0.03 counts the stuck timer down. With no point left, state 6 turns it the way it walked (`Combatant.FaceAndSettle`), and then, with `NextState` 8, `EnemyAI.FleeAdvance`: for an enemy not fleeing, `Execute`. The timer run out does the same from wherever it is.
6. **The action** (`EnemyAI.Execute`, `0x8005B594`): a weapon swap first if its style wants the other kind (`ChooseWeapon`, `0x8005A620`, which makes what it fights as, `+0x04`, what it wants, `+0x07`, and clears the selected spell's source, `ce+0x3D`; `SwapWeapon`, `0x8005A560`: "%s changes weapons", and the turn's spent resting); then (`EnemyAI.PrepareAction`, `0x8005B1D8`) Fireball's centre (`AimAreaSpell`, below) and both target sets from where it stands (`EnemyAI.PrepareTargetSets`, `0x8005B8F8`): for one that fights as a caster (`+0x04` 2) the spell's target first (`SelectSpellTarget`, `0x8005B878`: its target if set B has it selected, else B's cursor, set A emptied), then the weapon's; for any other the weapon's first (`SelectWeaponTarget`, `0x8005B7C8`: its target selected alone if set A has it, else whoever set A selected, set B emptied), then the spell's; and if it can act on that one (`EnemyAI.CanActOnTarget`, `0x8005B218`, which makes it the action's target, `+0x14`: a caster `CanCast` or `CanAttack`, the rest the other way round), it turns to it (`Combatant.FaceCombatant`, state 6) and attacks or casts through `Combatant.ExecuteAction` as the player does: state 6 attacks if set A has the target selected, else casts if set B has (nothing sets the AI's cast margin `+0x3C` first; `PrepareCast` rolls it). Otherwise it rests: state 24, 120 ticks (`Combat.EnterRest`), and the turn ends. So a caster whose weapon fallback has once won (styles 6 and 9 want melee, so `+0x04` becomes 0) is weapon-first from then on: with anyone in its weapon's reach it attacks, even when a spell scored best, as no fallback makes it a caster again.

**The styles** score with these: `ListOpponents` (`0x8005783C`, one entry per opponent in slot order: alive, able and on the other side); `ScoreByDistance` (`0x80058B90`, the grid's distance from the AI's cell, `ArenaGrid.Distance`, a square root truncated); `AreaDanger` (`0x80058A48`, 5 for each ally within the party's area danger `+0x12`, while the leader lives); `CollectAttackCells` (`0x80057908`, each free cell in its field from which set A has someone, with the nearest of those) and `CollectAttackCellsByReach` (`0x80057C30`, one cell per opponent, preferring cells no opponent's field reaches, then nearer); `ScoreAttackCells` (`0x80058C7C`, round each opponent the free cells from which set A has it: 25 its own cell, 20 the flank or back, `Combatant.IsFlanking`, 50 above it, up to 15 the more it's hurt, 10 if it holds a ranged weapon); `FarthestCell` (`0x800587BC`, the free cell in its field with the greatest summed distance from the opponents). The cell collectors try each cell by moving the combatant's place there and building set A, but `Combatant.IsWithinReach` reads both bodies again, so for melee reach only where its body stands counts: a clever melee fighter (style 1) far off finds no cell, and takes an opponent's own cell, the one the sort puts first.

| Style | Who | Chooses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | melee, Intelligence under 15 | the nearest opponent; its cell |
| 1, 4 | melee, clever; a weapon for near and far | each opponent's best cell (`ScoreAttackCells`), the best first |
| 2 | ranged, Intelligence under 15 | the nearest cell to attack from; with none, the nearest for the other kind of weapon, which it then wants; with none either, the nearest opponent |
| 3 | ranged, clever | with an opponent in reach, its best cell in melee, wanting melee; holding a melee weapon with none near, the farthest cell, wanting its bow; else the nearest cell to shoot from that no opponent's field reaches, or if all are reached the farthest; with none, style 0's |
| 5–10 | casters and bosses | their spells first (`EnemyAI.ChooseSpellOrAttack`, below), changing neither what they fight as nor what they want; with none that scores: 5 as 0, 6 its collected attack cells scored (or style 1), wanting melee, 7 as 3, 8 as 4, 9 as 6 over the opponents, 10 as 3 with the farthest cell as its fallback and the nearest order |

**The casters' spells** (`EnemyAI.ChooseSpellOrAttack`, `0x800590B0`; `ScoreSpellCells`, `0x800581C8`). Every action slot with a spell, in slot order, is scored from every free cell within the field (the square about its cell, Z outer and X inner, within `ArenaGrid.Distance` of the radius, free for its footprint with its own taken off, so its own cell counts): the combatant's place across the ground is moved there, the slot's spell selected and set B built for it there, and if B selects anyone and the spell has a value (`EnemyAI.SpellValue`, `0x80057498`, [magic](magic.md)), an entry of the cell, the slot + 1, B's cursor (the last it selected in slot order, or the combatant's last target or B's old cursor where `TargetSet.RestoreCursor` keeps it) and the score **(value + 10 × (selected − 1) + the spell's known level) & 0xFF** (the spellbook's level, or the item spell's, not the casting level). For Fireball (spell kind 8, and no other), the count is `EnemyAI.AreaCentre` (`0x80058008`): the area's centre (`Combat+0x18/+0x1C`) put at the mean place of the opponents, B built about it, and with anyone selected, +1 for each candidate of the other side and −1 for each of its own, itself included; a negative count wraps the byte, so a Fireball that would catch more of its own side than the other scores above everything. The list holds `(r² × 4 × 8) & 0xFFE0` entries, 32 for an engaged caster, and drops the rest. With a field of 0 (Pochanargat) only its own cell is scored (`ScoreSpellsInPlace`, `0x800585B0`). With any entry: sorted best first, the best's ties kept (`KeepBest`), each scored again by its distance (`ScoreByDistance` with the allies' area danger), and sorted nearest first: of the cells tied for the best spell, the nearest. `RunStyle` (`0x80059628`) then selects the entry's slot's spell. A slot the caster can't afford has no candidates (`AICanCast`, [Spells, potions and skills in a turn](#spells-potions-and-skills-in-a-turn)), so no entry; the flag `ScoreSpellCells` returns for it nothing reads.

**Buffing ahead** (the user's note in [engine-behaviour](../engine-behaviour.md#enemy-ai)) is the values: a spell for its own side is worth 51–85 (Darkness 85, Haste 73, Mirror 72, the `vs.` spells 71, the shields 61, the stat spells 51–56), a debuff or a hold 31–43 and a damage spell 11–22, and the count adds 10 a target more, so a Starlight Shield on the caster and an ally (61 + 10 + 5 = 76) beats Web of Starlight (48) and Lightning on three (36). A buff isn't cast at one already under it (`Casting`'s test of the target), so once its side has it, it attacks or debuffs. Every cell that reaches its allies scores the same, so it casts from where it stands. **Checked**: 118 cases of 21 casters and bosses laid over the checkpoints' rats, run in 1.0's own code.

**Fireball's aim** (`EnemyAI.AimAreaSpell`, `0x8005974C`, before the target sets, for kind 8 alone): of the cells of the arena within the spell's range byte × its casting level (3 × level) of the caster, Z outer and X inner, the first with the most candidates of the other side less its own, more than 0, becomes the centre; with none, the cell of `AreaCentre`'s mean. The blast's own reach about that centre is the casting level (`Combatant.CanCast`).

**The bosses** (`EnemyAI.HasBossStyle`, `0x8005BA40`, whose one caller is `EnemyAI.Init`) differ only in their styles' weapon fallbacks, 9 for 6 and 10 for 8; they score their spells as any caster. The 13: the six Bandit Bosses, the Chaos Major, Kitarak, the Lizard Man Boss, Pochanargat, Shamsuk, Lugash and the Wight.

**The sort** (`AiScores.Sort`, `0x8005BFC4`) is the game's own quicksort (`Quicksort.Sort`, `0x800B3978`, `quicksort.cpp`), not the C library's: three elements or two by comparisons, more by a pivot drawn at random and Hoare's partition. Its draws are on a stream of its own (`Quicksort.Seed`, `0x800E6B60`), 0 at boot, never seeded or saved, one draw for each partition of four or more, so a rat choosing among four draws once. Equal scores, common with distances truncated, fall in the order it draws. Nothing else in the AI's choice is random, and its turn draws nothing on the main stream but the action's own.

**Stuck, and resting for no visible reason** (the user's notes in [engine-behaviour](../engine-behaviour.md#enemy-ai)), all the original's:

- The planner sees the grid alone: a cell can be free and gentle there and still blocked for the body (a wall's edge, a narrow gap), or held by the push of another body. The body then comes no nearer, gives up after 30 ticks where it stands, and the enemy acts or rests from there.
- The combatant stands on its leg's end at once, so enemies planning later route round a place its body may never reach; every combatant reads its body again as the next turn begins.
- A rest, 120 ticks with no line: the greedy path fails (a ring of the party and its own allies round the target, anything concave), and the target isn't in reach where it stands; the first corner can't be walked; after the leg nothing is in reach (cut short by the field, the give-up or the timer, or an engaged enemy's single cell); a weapon swap; a flight's leg.

**Morale** (`EnemyAI.CheckFlee`, `0x800572AC`, as an AI's turn begins, `TurnOrder.BeginTurn`): every AI combatant but the leader while it lives is asked; with morale under 12 and a check called for (`EnemyAI.MayCheckMorale`, `0x8005722C`: a check due, or no Stamina, or not already fleeing and no more than half the enemies that began able), the leader's morale is worked out first while it lives (`+0x11`), then its own (`EnemyAI.Morale`, `0x80056FA8`, which clears the check due), against 2d6 on the main stream: rolled over, it flees (`EnemyAI.StartFleeing`, `0x8005B9E4`: four turns, unless it's engaged or the arena has no flee points), otherwise it stops fleeing. Its morale, from 2 to 11:

| a weakness has hit it (flag `0x10`, set with the check due by `ApplyResistance`) | −2 |

**Fleeing** (`EnemyAI.BeginFleeWalk`, `0x8005B038`): each of its turns says "%s flees" and heads for the nearest flee point (`EnemyAI.NearestFleePoint`, `0x8005AE3C`, the last of equals), as any walk: one leg, bounded by its field. Within 1.5 of the point it leaves at once. After the leg (`EnemyAI.FleeAdvance`, `0x8005B434`) its turns count down, and it leaves on the last, within 1.5 of a flee point, or if it hasn't got a unit from where its turn began; then it rests. With no path, it walks straight to a flee point within 2.25 (`EnemyAI.FleeStep`, `0x8005AA3C`). With no flee point at all it stops fleeing and chooses as any other. **`Combatant.Leave`** (`0x80061480`): it leaves the grid, flag 6 (gone), the counts of those able fall, its effects and potions come off, its body fades out over 120 ticks (`Body.FadeOut`, `0x80014AD4`), `Combat.Bodies` is emptied at once, and its portrait is marked fled. It isn't dead: the kill flags aren't touched, and it still pays its experience and chest.

**Reserves that flee as they arrive** (`EnemyAI.ReserveFleesOnArrival`, `0x80056E18`, inside `Combat.SpawnReserves` as a dead or gone combatant's slot is to be refilled): only where there are flee points, with morale under 12, and while **more** than half the enemies that began are still able; its record's morale, less 1 for its aspect (any but Solar counting as Lunar), 4 with the leader gone or unable, and the leader's morale's say, from 2 to 11, against 2d6. Fleeing, it's passed over (`+0x07` moves on), its small portrait marked (`CombatGui.MarkReserveFled`), its slot left as it was until the next round, and `+0x543C` set: the next turn's change shows "Enemy reinforcements flee" and the whole battle holds 60 ticks (`Combat.HoldForMessage`, `0x80022AEC`). `Combat.SkipFleeingReserves` has no caller.

**Sholeh** with an AI record plays her turns the same way; on the party's side she never counts as fleeing.

## Spells, potions and skills in a turn

Read from 1.0's code, checked by running the original's own functions on checkpoints 05 and 07 and against 08, and replayed by the port (`SpellCheckpointTests`, `FlaskCheckpointTests`).

**The held menus** (`CombatMenu`, `0x80087C60`–`0x80089780`). `Combat.Controls` gives each poll's event to `CombatMenu.Input` (`0x80087C8C`) before the state's own: in states 1, 9, 10, 11, 18 and 25, a C-up press opens C-up's menu (`CombatMenu.OpenItems`, `0x80087F2C`, state 23) and a C-down press C-down's (`OpenSpells`, `0x80088028`, state 22), C-up winning a poll that has both. Opening puts an aim away (`Combat.CancelToTurn`, `0x80083C2C`: `ReturnToTurn`, and `RevertAction` from 9) and stands the body. The menu is a `ChoicePopup` that isn't in the widget manager, so it comes and goes on the frame, with no fade:

- **Moving**: the stick's or the D-pad's up and down move the cursor a row, wrapping, held repeating after 30 polls and then every 5 (`MenuStick.Step`, `0x800AD708`). A, B, Z, L, R, Start and the C buttons do nothing.
- **Choosing**: letting the button go (`CombatMenu.Close`, `0x80088118`) sets state 1 and frees the menu, whose destructor calls the cursor's row's callback (`DestroySpellsMenu` `0x80088F90`, `DestroyItemsMenu` `0x800895A0`), so the callback's state wins. The row is remembered by party slot (`CombatMenu.SpellCursors` `0x800E3180`, `ItemCursors` `0x800E3190`, reset at the battle's start) and the cursor starts there next time, clamped to the list.
- **C-down's rows** (`CombatMenu.BuildSpellsMenu`, `0x80088B58`): Lay On Hands while modified Healer isn't 0 and modified Stamina is at least max(1, 11 − Healer) (`Entity.CanLayOnHands`, `0x80071ED0`); Heal With Herbs with a Herb in the party's inventory and Stamina at least max(1, 5 − Healer) (`CanHealWithHerbs`, `0x80071F38`); Perform while modified Troubadour isn't 0; then the spells known, sorted by name with `strcmp` (so "vs. Necromancy" comes last), each listed if its reagent is in the party's inventory or it needs none, and not Photosynthesis at night. Nothing else is tested: a spell out of reach, too dear or for the field is listed and fails later. The skills are blue (0, 128, 225, 225) with the Healer's cross `0x0185` or the lute `0x018C`; the spells red (225, 80, 80, 225) with their school's icon (`Spell.SchoolIcons`, `0x800DEFC0`: Chaos `0x01A9`, Elemental `0x01A5`, Naming `0x01A6`, Necromancy `0x01A7`, Star `0x01A8`, none for the unschooled). No level, cost or count shows.
- **C-up's rows** (`BuildItemsMenu`, `0x800891B4`): one row a kind of potion the party holds, in the kinds' order, that `Potions.CanDrink` allows now (never a flask or Stealth), green (0, 200, 0, 225) with the flask `0x35E5`; then the weapon's, shield's, body armor's and gear slots' spells with charges left (`CombatMenu.HasCharges`, `0x800885E0`), yellow (200, 200, 0, 225) with the item's own image.
- **Empty**, a message instead: "No spells or skills available" or "No potions or item spells available" (code strings, importer 45), which releasing closes.
- **The look**: the conversation box's feathered black panel round a list 120 wide centred on x 160, as tall as its rows up to 95 (55 without the Expansion Pak) and centred on y 120; each row its icon at its left and its text after it, at the menu's icon width + 5 (10 for C-down's, 16 for C-up's) and half that width less half the text's height down, the rows stacked with no gap; the cursor's row pulsing from (100, 100, 100, 100) toward its colour over 20 ticks and back (`WidgetPulseMenu.Draw`, `0x8002809C`), with no box; the arrows `0x38D0` and `0x38CF` 8 left of the list while it scrolls, 10 pixels a tick. Checked: the rows of every member at checkpoint 07.

**The selected spell** persists: `Combatant.Init` (`0x8005EAE0`) selects the first spell a combatant knows as each is made, and a menu's choice changes it. `Combat.EndTurn` puts the cast's margin (`+0x3C`) to `0xFF` but keeps the choice; a refusal for the reagent, the Stamina or an item's charges (`Combatant.FailSpell`, `0x800636A4`), a spent scroll or wand and an equipment change with a spell (`Entity.ApplyItem`) clear it. Set B is built for it at every turn's start and as the combatant moves, so its reach tints the portraits red whether or not a menu opens.

**Target set B** (`TargetSet.Rebuild` in mode 1; `FindSpellTargets`, `0x800693C4`): every combatant `Combatant.CanCast` (`0x80060C38`) allows, and as many may be chosen as `SpellTargetCount` (`0x80060818`) gives: every combatant in play for an All spell (then every candidate), (level + 1) >> 1 for a ByLevel one, else one. `CanCast`:

1. Never by or at the Shadow; `SpellCanTarget` (`0x80060988`): alive, not gone, on the caster's side for a Party spell and the other for an Enemy one (Area and All either, so Fireball takes the party too), not under the spell already, its requirement met, and `SpellKindAllowsTarget` (`0x8006088C`, the plugin's filter: the Elementals for Control Elemental and Banishing, no Chaos for Charming, the Marquis, the Zombies); an AI's own tests (`AICanCast`: the cost, a charge, no requirement, the time).
2. Teleportation and a Self spell take the caster alone.
3. Its reach: the effective level × the spell's range byte, from the caster's place, to the target's body, its scaled radius added (`InSpellRange`, `0x80060B60`); a placed area's is the level alone, from the placed point (`Combat+0x18/+0x1C`). Sense Aura has none.
4. Its line: `WalkClear` from the caster's (or the point's) cell, or the same cell, or `HasSight` from 2.0 above the floor under that cell (`0x800D28A0`, a sphere of 0.5 swept from 50 to −10) to the target's head, only the target's body excluded.

`Rebuild` selects in slot order. A menu's choice calls `TargetSet.BeginSpell` (`0x80069574`) instead: a spell of one target orders the candidates by angle as an attack does and starts on the last target if it's one, else the first of that order (the camera on the first); one of several selects them all if they're fewer than it takes, else the nearest to the caster's body (`SelectNearest`, `0x80069D90`, a later equal first), and the player chooses nothing. **Checked**: 07's set B for Rheda's Endurance (ByLevel, level 4) is the four members as candidates with her and Alaron, the two nearest, selected; and every member's spells give the candidates 1.0's `BeginSpell` gives.

**State 10** (`Combat.InputSpellTarget`, `0x800844BC`): Start pauses back to state 1; B, Z or L put it away; left steps a single target's cursor +1 and right −1, wrapping (`TargetSet.StepSpellOne`, `0x800696D4`), the camera to it and the caster's body turning to it (`Combat.AimAtCursor`); A is `Combat.ConfirmSpellTarget` (`0x80083F58`): set A emptied, set B's candidates cleared, the **highest** selected slot the first target (none: back to the turn), the camera to the caster, the margin 0, and the caster turns to it (state 6) or, casting at itself, state 6 at once.

**State 11** (`Combat.InputAreaTarget`, `0x80084964`), entered with the centre at the caster's place: the stick alone (not the D-pad) moves it 0.8 a frame, up being the camera's way (`Combat.MoveAreaCursor`, `0x8008465C`), on the grid and within the range × the level of the caster's body, and set B is built again about it; state 11's camera is 8 and 16 about the centre. A is `ConfirmAreaTarget` (`0x80084184`), as state 10's, but Teleportation's point must be clear (`Combat.SpotIsClear`, `0x8005D474`: no living body's centre within its radius and the caster's), or nothing happens. `Entity.Teleport` (`0x80071978`) reads the point itself.

**The marker** (`Combat.DrawTargetCounts`, `0x80021448`, every frame) draws one translucent cylinder, scene `0x0797`, at the focus, set on the floor by a sphere of 1 swept down, scaled (r + 0.2, 2, r + 0.2) at the scene colours' alpha 64 (`CombatSpellFx.DrawNumber`, `0x8008ABD8`): red with r the reach about the caster in state 10, red with r the level about the point in 11 (1 for Teleportation), green with r 1 at a flask's aim in 18. It draws no numbers.

**The cast** (`Combatant.ExecuteAction`'s spell branch and `Combatant.CastCurrentSpell`, `0x80065C08`), a target at a time: set A's selection cleared; `CastSpell` at the target, the first preparing it and the rest sharing its roll, reagent and cost (the acted flag); a damage of 0 shows "%s casts %s" and queues "resists"; the caster's body plays request 5 and `CombatSpellFx.Start` (`0x8008C43C`) its visual, even for a refusal; then as an attack, `CheckDeath`, the damage on the target, the glide and state 2. State 2 waits for the caster's request 5 to end and 30 ticks, then the target is hit (request 7 and the number), or a same-side one helped (10) and an opponent dodges (8); state 4's `RemoveFallen` takes the target off both sets and, with more selected, turns the caster to the **first** selected by slot (state 6), until none is left and the turn ends. **A spell never goes through state 3**, which only arrows, thrown weapons and flasks take. A cast whose margin is below 0 (refused or failed) casts at no more targets for a player. The lines: "%s casts %s" shown, "%s cast on %s" queued and flushed at state 2's end; the failures shown and queued; a requirement's refusal "%s" with the Common strings' lines 258–270 (`Spell.CastMessages`, `0x800E2C00`, a pointer the string loader sets). **Checked**: replaying 07's A gives 08's state 2, target, margin 4, acted flag, lines and two draws, then Alaron with no draw.

**Potions and flasks** (`Combatant.ThrowFlask`, `0x80065600`, action 3):

- **A potion** is drunk (`Combatant.DrinkPotion`, `0x80064C84`, [magic](magic.md#potions-and-flasks)): a heal says what it gained (shown and queued) or that it failed, Stamina that it's full, the rest that it took; no number shows. The body plays request 5, one is used up and the action goes back.
- **A flask in hand** is action 3 with the kind and `+0x8A`; it stays in the party's inventory. It's taken in hand from the pause menu's Party tab (below), which ends the turn and stows the weapon (`Party.StowHeldItem`, `0x800763B0`, at the turn's end): the member is left unarmed. Each turn's start lets it go if the party has none left.
- **Aiming** is state 18 with set A: the other side, any distance (`Combatant.WeaponReach` is 400).
- **The throw**: `ThrowChance` (`0x80064EFC`): the thrower's modified Intelligence and Dexterity, base Level, 6 × Thrown, 2 × Warrior and 40, less half the target's shield defence, its modified Dexterity and base Level; less (distance − 5) past 6; 20 from behind; +10 at a target with no Stamina, −20 at one that has moved or acted, −25 having moved; the night's −30 and fog's −10 or a storm's −20 with the distance again (`Combat.WeatherPenalty`, `0x80062D78`); the aspect and the Troubadour; at least 5. A d100 that Warrior turns. A hit draws 4d6, 8d6 and 2d6 always and takes its own (`FlaskDamage`, `0x80065270`; Sleep Gas 15), shows and queues "%s attacks %s", keeps the damage on the thrower (`+0x29`) and the target's cell (`Combat+0x5444`), and trains Thrown on a d100; a miss queues "%s misses" on half the training. Either way request 15 and one used up (`ConsumeFlask`, `0x80064AB0`, which puts the action back): the next of the stack stays in hand, and **the last empties the hand at once, so it never flies or bursts**: its target dodges. The port fixes this, the user's choice (below).
- **The flight** is an arrow's (state 3), scene `0x34C5` to the target's feet, the muzzle number 4 and a spin of 8 a tick.
- **The blast** (`Combat.EndCast` → `Combatant.FlaskExplosion`, `0x800652F8`): about the landing cell, each living combatant at a squared distance d **kept in a byte** (so 544 counts as 32) and at least 1, within the damage, takes damage ÷ d (from Stamina for Sleep Gas), the others left with at least 1, its number shown at once; the target's loss is queued and its death checked; Acid's corrosion draws 2d6 and those within it lose radius ÷ d protection for the battle instead. Then `EndCast` lands the target's share as a blow: **the target takes it twice**. **Ran** at 05: a Fire flask of 10 takes a rat from 31 to 21, then 11; of 40, a rat 20 squared away loses 2 and members 23 cells away 1.

**The Troubadour** (state 25, `Combat.InputTroubadour` `0x8008508C`: no text, set A holding the caster; A is `Combatant.StartTroubadour`, `0x800662F4`): with a performance already going in the battle, "%s is already performing" and the turn goes on; with no Stamina, "%s is too weak to perform"; else (Troubadour × 5 + Intelligence) × 2 against a d100: made, the bonus (`Combat+0x13`) is the margin's step, at least 1, 3 Stamina go, "%s performs" and the lute on the body; failed, "%s fails to perform". Then request 5 and state 26, 120 ticks, and the turn ends. The skill trains on a d100 at or under a tenth of (8 − skill)² (8 from 6), halved on a failure (`GainSmallExperience`, `0x8006620C`). The performer's turns say "%s continues to perform", and it pays a Stamina point each round's end. A performance ends as it acts, changes weapon or action, takes a potion or healing in hand, is engaged or dies, or with no Stamina at a round's end (`StopPerforming`, `0x80066100`, which takes the bonus back).

**The Healer** (`Combatant.ResolveHealer`, `0x8006591C`): set A's healing candidates (`CanAttack`'s branch): the caster's side, hurt, within melee reach and sight; Lay On Hands never the caster, Heal With Herbs the caster with no test. Healed: request 5, "%s heals %s" shown and queued, "gains %d hit points" queued, and the healing number (`+0x2A`) after the target's helped request; else "%s fails to perform healing". No skill experience.

**Sense Aura** (`Entity.SenseAura`, `0x80070070`) puts up the Party tab's message box with code strings by the cast's level: the target's name; from 1 "Leader" or "Follower"; from 3 "%d Total Hitpoints"; from 5 "Level %lu"; from 7 its resistances, "Immune to %s" at 0 or less, "Resistant to %s" under 2.0, else "Vulnerable to %s"; from 9 "Total Armor Protection %d" (importer 45). **Escape** (`Combatant.Leave` on each target while the party can flee) takes a member off the field as a fleeing enemy goes; its body gone, state 2 waits for it for ever, a softlock (by the code and 1.0's own functions run; Escape is reached only by cheating: Ardra, who knows it, never fights, and its scroll isn't in the game). ## What it shares with trek

- **The zone engine**, in mode 1, framed each combat frame (`Combat.DrawFrame`) and freed after: the chunks, collision, scenes, lights, sky, rain and the terrain (`World.SetTerrain` every frame), with trek built again after. The port's `ZoneEngine` was made for an arena to be a stage as a cut-scene's is.
- **Bodies**: `AllocPlayer` and `FreePlayer`, `Body.SetDown`, `Body.Face`, the player handler with a goal for the AI's walks, shadows to 128, and the actors' requests (7 hit, 9 death).

**The combat strings** (String/Combat, 64 lines, [text](../formats/text.md)) are taken by byte offset: `0x00` "begins action", `0x04` the party flees, `0x08` victorious, `0x10`–`0x1C` healing, `0x20`–`0x38` avoid, attack, hit, critical, miss, `0x3C`–`0x4C` potions, `0x50` killed, `0x58`–`0x70` spells, `0x74` the Shadow, `0x78` flees, `0x7C` joins, `0x80` the vortex, `0x84` rests, `0x88` changes weapons, `0x8C`–`0xBC` a combatant's details (nothing in combat's files shows them), `0xC0`–`0xD0` performing, `0xD4` the party incapacitated, `0xD8` `flee%d`, `0xDC` `%s%u%c`, `0xE8` banished, `0xEC` "Enemy reinforcements flee", `0xF0` the Shadow dissipates, `0xF4` and `0xF8` `good` and `bad`, `0xFC` "cheats death".

**Where the port differs:**

- The camera acts every tick rather than once a frame; only its goal and holds are once-a-frame things, so it's the same at 30 fps.
- A bystander in the first four leaves the original a null slot that it counts as an enemy on the field; the port leaves it out and says so. No monster party that fights has one.

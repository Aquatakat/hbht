# Magic: spells, potions and enchantments (roadmap 2.5)

`HBHT.Core.Magic`: `Casting` (the roll, the resist, targets), `EffectLanding` (where every effect lands), `EffectRules` (what a lasting effect does each round and as it ends), `SpellValues` (the AI's) and `IMagicHost` (what an effect asks of the battle). The effects on a character are in `Character.Effects.cs` (`ActiveEffect`), drinking in `Potions` ([items](items.md#potions)) and a cast in a battle in `Battle.Magic.cs`. [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-one-effect-vocabulary.md) makes spells, potions and enchantments data: a record lists **effects**, each of a type the core implements, configured by parameters (`EffectRecords.cs` in `HBHT.Plugins`). [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-instant-effects-land-in-one-place.md) lands every carrier's effects through `EffectLanding`, and makes a potion's use and a spell's area the records'.

What the original does is from 1.0's machine code and the combat checkpoints. A name with an address is the original's, in the symbol map (`Entity.`, `Spell.`, `EffectRecord.`, `PotionRecord.` and `Combatant.` are coined); one without is the port's. Several of `Casting`'s members share their original's name.

## The records

- **A spell** (`aidyn:spell/0x03…`) has the spell DB's fields and its `effects`, its `duration` (1800 game-clock ticks a level for those that last, the battle for Wraith Touch, none for the rest), its `aspect` (the ROM's `unk1E` bits 1 and 2, [db](../formats/db.md)), who resists it, the `filter` of entities it may take, and its `appeal`, the base of the AI's score ([below](#the-ais-values)), all written as facts from 1.0's code (`MagicImporter`). Its `reagent` is the item each cast uses up, or none: the importer turns the DB's byte (spice, herb or gemstone) into the inventory's item for it (`InventoryRecord.Spice`, `Herb`, `Gemstone`, the table at `0x800E29CC`), so a mod's spell can use up any item.
- **A spell's `targeting`**: `type` (Field, Party, Enemy, Area or All) and `count` (Self, One, All or ByLevel) from the DB; `placed`, the caster places its area and the range is about that point (`unk1E` bit 0x10: Fireball, Teleportation and the four dispels); and `blast`, the AI centres the placed area on its opponents and a party member who knows the spell makes the enemies spread out ([combat-ai](combat-ai.md#the-casters-spells)). Only Fireball, kind 8, which the code names, is a blast (`EnemyAI.AreaCentre` `0x80058008`, `EnemyAI.AimAreaSpell` `0x8005974C`, `Combat.CreatePartyCombatants` `0x8005C7C8`).
- **A spell's or potion's `presentation`** (`MagicPresentation`): the actors played at the caster and the target, whether the target's stands at its head, the aura shown while the effect lasts ([combat-actions](combat-actions.md)), and a spell's `sound`, which the caster plays as its cast animation starts, a refused cast's too (`Player.SetCastSound`). Every spell has a sound; five (Detecting Traps, the two phase spells, Stealth, Poison) have nothing else. A potion's is its own, and has no sound: 1.0 plays none for one, and the port plays none if a mod gives one. The original's tables also give each actor the memory it asks for, which it skips the actor without; the port loads every actor and doesn't import them.
- **A potion** (`aidyn:item/0x10nn`) has its effects and duration: 648,000 ticks (`0x9E340`, 3 hours of game time) for the stat, skill and Defence potions. Its `use` says where it's used: `DrinkInTrek`, `DrinkInBattle`, `Throw` ([below](#potions-and-flasks)).
- **Gear** names a spell and a level: an item's `spell` is cast when used, with charges; an armor's or gear's `enchantment` is on while worn; a weapon's `enchantment` is cast at the target of each hit.
- **Schools and elements are records** (ADR 0033): a spell's `school` and a `ward`'s or `dispel`'s are `aidyn:school/n` ([characters](characters.md#the-records)), and a `damage` effect's `elements` are `aidyn:element/n`, whose `name` Sense Aura shows and whose `covers` say what a resistance to Physical or Magic meets on a blow. Which element meets which spell is still the core's ([hardcoded-content](../hardcoded-content.md)).
- **The Stealth potion's effects outlast a battle** (`Entity.ClearEffects`, `0x80071830`): its item's `keptAfterBattle`, which was the magic record's list.
- **The magic record** (`aidyn:magic/0`) holds casting's numbers. The chance's factors, the improvement factor and the resist roll's aspect factors are read from the code segment (`CodeTable.CastingFactors`, `0x800D29CC`, the same in all three ROMs).

## A spell's kind

The code keys a spell by its **kind**, its place in the spell table (`SpellList`, `0x800E2B40`; the record's `position`): `GetIdIndex` turns an ID into it, and the effect records, the AI's values and the special-case lists are all by kind. There are 59, IDs `0x0300`–`0x033F` with five gaps. The ROM's item records hold spell IDs; the item tables in RAM hold kinds. The port keys by record ID throughout, so modded spells need no kind.

## Casting

`Combatant.CastSpell` (`0x800648CC`: the caster, the target, the spell, and whether it's a weapon's as it strikes), once per target of the selection (`Combatant.CastCurrentSpell`, `0x80065C08`, with the selected spell as `Entity.CurrentSpellChecked` gives it, `0x80070D50`). It returns the damage, or `0xFFFD` for an effect with no number, `0xFFFF` resisted and `0xFFFE` not cast. The port's is `Battle.Cast(caster, source, targets)`, a target at a time through `CastAt`. The formula vectors run it 960 ways against the port's ([combat-parity](combat-parity.md#the-formula-vectors)).

1. **Available** (`0x800635B8`): a Field spell is "Spell Unavailable" (shown and queued, the cast's margin `0xFE`, the port's `Battle.MarginAfter`); the target must meet the spell's special (`Casting.Requirement`): a True Name, the heavy flag set (which the party, the rats and ordinary humanoids have and the 32 big creatures haven't, so "heavy" means small enough), or an aspect (`Entity.Requirement`, `0x800712C0`: special 1 a True Name, 2 heavy, 3 Solar, 4 Lunar), a refusal that leaves the margin as it was. Day and Night specials test the battle's part of the day; no spell has one. A player's refused cast shows "%s" with the requirement's line, the Common strings' 258–270 (`Spell.CastMessages`, `0x800E2C00`, a pointer the string loader sets; `Combatant.LogMissingComponent`, `0x800638C8`).
2. **The level** (`Casting.Level`): a known spell's level, no higher than the caster's modified Wizard; an item's spell its own.
3. **Prepared,** once (`PrepareCast`, `0x8006406C`, skipped while the acted flag is up):
   - a known spell (`AttemptCast`, `0x80063F7C`): the reagent (the spell's `+0x1D`, an item of `Spell.ReagentItems`, `0x800E29CC`: Spice `0x0111`, Herb `0x0112`, Gemstone `0x0113`) out of the party's inventory, unless the caster is from the enemy list or the AI plays it (`Combatant.CheckReagent`, `0x80063BFC`, "Missing spell component"); the cost, max(1, the spell's Stamina − L), from the worn items' batteries and then Stamina (`Entity.PaySpellCost`, `0x80070F18`, the port's `Equipment.PayStamina`, "Not enough ST", the reagent kept); then the roll. A battery is a worn item whose stat bonus is Stamina's, drawn in place, the weapon's first, then the body armor's, the shield's and the gear's by slot (`Entity.DrawBattery`, `0x800710A8`, which reads its charge signed); what's left comes off Stamina's current and modified values, its base untouched, and a cost of exactly the Stamina left is paid. Both refusals clear the selected spell and set the margin `0xFE` (`Combatant.FailSpell`, `0x800636A4`). Everyone pays Stamina, the AI's casters too;
   - an item's spell, from C-up's menu, whatever its item: a charge left or "fails to cast the spell" (`Entity.SpellCharges`, `0x80070D84`), which only a wand or a scroll worn as gear spends (`Entity.UseCharge`, `0x80070E0C`), so the weapon's from the menu needs a charge and spends none; no reagent, Stamina or roll: the margin is a d100's step.
4. **The roll** (`Casting.Chance`, `0x80063CA0`): (modified Intelligence × 4 + L × 6 + base Level, the magic record's `chance`: `intelligence`, `level`, `casterLevel`) × a factor, the moon's phase's for a Lunar caster (0.5, 0.75, 1.25, 1.5, by `World+3`, which `World.UpdateMoonPhase`, `0x8007BC70`, sets from the calendar), the battle's part of the day's for anyone else (0.75, 1.25, 1.25, 0.75, 0.75 from morning to night), truncated, times the Troubadour's scale, at least 5. A d100 at or under the modified Wizard, and under a fifth of the chance, has a second d100 taken off it (`Casting.AdjustRoll`, `0x80062594`). Under the chance succeeds, with the margin the step of what's left (`Party.SkillCheckStep`, `0x80008654`); otherwise the reagent comes back (to any caster not from the enemy list: the refund at `0x80063EB8` doesn't ask whether the AI plays it, as `CheckReagent` does, so such a caster would be given one it never paid; read, not run, and no such caster is known), "fails to cast the spell", the refused cast's puff of smoke plays at the caster ([combat-actions](combat-actions.md#what-spells-look-like)), and the next target tries again with another. Cast or not, a spell at level 0 or 1 may rise a level: a d100 at or under (5 − L)² × 0.1, halved on a failure (`ResolveCastRoll`, `0x80063DF4`).
5. **Resisted** (`Casting.Resist`, `0x80064298`), by everyone for a spell at enemies or a placed area, by the other side for Wind, by no one for the rest, and never by the Marquis for Wall of Bones: (modified Willpower × 3 + modified Endurance + base Level) plus its ward, × 0.75 for a Solar or Lunar spell when the two share an aspect or 1.25 when they don't. A d100 over it doesn't resist; otherwise its step comes off the margin, and a step of the margin or more resists, as does any while Mirror's counter lasts, which spends the difference.
6. **What it does** (`Combatant.ApplySpellEffect`): the disable limit, then the effects for the target's side land (`EffectLanding.Land`, [below](#how-effects-land)), the lasting ones as a record; the lines say a Banishing, a dispel that took nothing, or the cast. **The disable limit** (`Combatant.DisableLimit`, `0x80063A00`): while reserves are still to come (`Combat+7` isn't 12), a charm or a hold is blocked, with the resists line, once three in the enemies' slots (`Combat+3` up to `Combat+1`) are charmed or held and alive. It holds the charms (4, 16, 17, 27) back when the party's side casts them (`CheckDisableLimit`, `0x80063B10`), and the holds (34, 53, 57) whoever casts them (`CheckImmobilisingLimit`, `0x80063B9C`), so an enemy's Web at a party member is blocked too while three enemies are held. The port's (`Battle.TooManyDisabled`) limits the party's side alone; which to keep is open ([below](#not-settled)). The blocked cast still does damage ([below](#the-originals-quirks-the-port-keeps)).
7. **Damage:** (margin + the dice) d6, times every resistance of the target's and its worn items' that meets the spell (`Combatant.SpellDamage`, `0x800649E4`; `SpellResistanceApplies`: the damage's elements, the spell's school's, its aspect's, or Magic, the port's `Casting.ResistanceMultiplier`): at least 1, unless a resistance is 0. A product over 1, a weakness, makes the morale check of a target the AI plays due, as a blow's does (its AI record's flags `0x12`, `0x80064A84`): the Tomb Rats' Fire ×1.5 under Fireball and Dragon Flames. `Battle.Cast` returns it in the outcome; the state that shows the numbers deals it.

**Targets**: who a cast may take, how many and from how far (`Casting.TargetCount`, `CanTarget`, `Reach`) are target set B's, [combat-actions](combat-actions.md#spells-potions-and-skills-in-a-turn).

**A weapon's spell** (`Combatant.WeaponSpellProc`, `0x800627D0`; `Battle.Strike`) is cast after every melee and ranged hit when it may take the target: an item's spell at the enchantment's level, its damage added to the hit's. Cast as any spell, its roll's step is kept as the attacker's cast margin (`+0x3C`, `PrepareCast`'s `0x80064184`) until the turn ends, and only a later cast in the same turn reads it (below). The spell is the weapon record's `+0x1C`, its place in the spell list (`0x800E2B40`) and its level, built on the stack and freed after (`Spell.Free`, `0x800728D8`). **No charge is spent or needed**: an item's `PrepareCast` takes its d100 at `0x800640B4` and never reaches `Entity.UseCharge`, which the port matches (`SpellSource.Proc`). An attacker that has acted this turn (`+0x2C` bit 0) skips `PrepareCast`'s d100 and casts at the margin it kept. The formula vectors run it for twelve weapons ([combat-parity](combat-parity.md#the-formula-vectors)).

## How effects land

`EffectLanding.Land` is the one place a spell's, a potion's or a flask's effects land on a target (ADR 0021), so a plugin's spell that heals or potion that dispels does what the base game's carriers do. In the record's order:

- **Once:** `damage` adds its dice and `banish` the target's hit points to the d6 over the margin, which the carrier rolls (the cast through the resistances; drinking and a flask as that many d6, no margin); `dispel` (`Entity.Dispel`, `0x80071CD8`), `cure` (`Entity.CurePoison`, `0x80071A6C`) and `heal` (`Entity.HealPercent`, `0x800715DC`, none in the Shadow's battle: `Battle.HealingBarred`) act on the sheet; `flee`, `teleport`, `senseAura` and `revealPhase` ask the battle (`IMagicHost`), and do nothing in trek.
- **A lasting effect on a record with no duration** starts and leaves nothing: the Stamina potion's Stamina, the base game's one.
- **`blast` and `corrode`** burst together about a cell (`IMagicHost.Burst`, [below](#potions-and-flasks)): where a flask landed, or the target's cell for a spell or a drunk potion. A flask's blast has the damage its throw rolled; anyone else's rolls its dice as it lands.
- **With a duration**, the lasting effects go on the target as one record (`Character.AddEffect`), Tap Stamina's gain on the caster as another.

A spell lands at its cast's level and margin. A potion or flask lands at level 0 and **margin 255** (`Potions.Margin`), which its dispel and cure weigh, as the original's Antidote adds its Remove Poison at 255; its lasting record keeps margin 0, as the original's potion record has none. A dispel of the unschooled school takes nothing off.

## Effects on a character

**The record** (the original's `EffectRecord`, 0x18 bytes, 15 at `entity+0x34`): `+0` kind, `+1` power (L), `+2` the spell's school, `+3` the margin after the resist, `+4` u32 harmful, `+8` u32 timer in game-clock ticks (`0xFFFFFFFF` never runs down), `+0x14` the visual's handle, whose end ends its lasting scene ([combat-actions](combat-actions.md#what-spells-look-like)). **Potions'** (the original's `PotionRecord`, 0xC bytes, 7 at `entity+0x38`): kind, power, u32 timer, the visual. The port's is `ActiveEffect`, held in two slot arrays (`Character.Effects`, `PotionEffects`), as their order is the RNG's. The combat checkpoints 08–12 hold live records and their timers.

- **Adding** (`Character.AddEffect`): the first free slot. A spell already on, or one with no slot free, adds nothing and doesn't refresh, though the cast still costs; targeting rules it out first. A potion isn't checked: drinking checks.
- **Ageing** (`Character.AgeEffects`, the original's `0x80071444`): the potions, then the spells (`EffectRules.Round`). A combat round takes 600 off each timer, 10 seconds of game time (the original's `TurnOrder.EndRound`, `0x8006A62C`, for every combatant in play; the port's `Battle.AgeEffects`). Trek takes the clock's 72 a tick of play, not while the pause menu is open (the original's `TrekLoop.TickClock`, `0x80020cb4`; the port's `GameState.PassTime`), so a potion's 3 hours last 9,000 ticks of play. A timer at 0 ends on the next ageing, before its round's effect, and one of 600 or less goes to 0 in a round, so a cast of L lasts 3L rounds and its per-round effects run 3L times (`ActiveEffect.Age`, `0x8007ABD8`: a spell's timer of `0xFFFFFFFF` never runs down; a potion's, `PotionRecord.Age` `0x80079DF4`, has no such exemption). A dead character's don't age. A round's damage that would be more than the hit points is rolled and not dealt; one that kills goes through `Character.TakeDamage`, Cheat Death and all, and a poison's doesn't (it comes off Endurance's current value). The formula vectors run each kind over its rounds ([combat-parity](combat-parity.md#the-formula-vectors)).
- **Ending** (`Character.RemoveEffect`, `0x800724B8`): each effect undone (`EffectRules.End`).
- **Damage** on a combatant ends its performance first, whether or not it kills (`Character.TakeDamage`, `Combatant.StopPerforming` `0x80066100`).
- **Death** (`Character.TakeDamage`): a Cheat Death on the dead heals twice its margin (`& 0x7FFF`, shifted) and ends; otherwise, in a battle, every spell's record comes off, and every potion's, and if that brought the character back its Endurance, Stamina and Level are put to 0 (`0x80071E28`). Taking a stat back does nothing to the dead, so a buff on the dead stays raised ([below](#the-originals-quirks-the-port-keeps)).
- **A battle's end** (`Character.EndBattleEffects`, the original's `Entity.ClearEffects` `0x80071830`): the potions but Stealth's (`MagicRecord.KeptAfterBattle`), every spell record, then the armor's, shield's and gear's enchantments back on.
- **What reads them** besides the table's rules: `Combatant.IsImmobilised` (`0x80062250`: any record of kind 34, 53 or 57, the list at `0x800E0C38` through `IsImmobilisingKind`, `0x800639AC`), which an attack's chance and whether a combatant can act ask ([combat](combat.md#a-turn)), and the AI's morale, −1 for any harmful record.

## Potions and flasks

A potion is data (ADR 0021): an item of kind Potion whose record has its effects and its `use`. The core has no list of them.

**Drinking** (`Potions.Drink`; trek's `0x8006FE34`, combat's `Combatant.DrinkPotion` `0x80064C84`) lands the potion's effects on the drinker ([above](#how-effects-land)) and uses one up. Whether it can (`Potions.CanDrink`, `0x8006FBE8`) follows from the record: its `use` must allow it here (`DrinkInTrek` from the pause menu, `DrinkInBattle` from C-up's), and a flask (`Throw`) or a potion with no effects (Aspect, Jumping) never; then a lasting one if it isn't working already; otherwise a heal below full health, a cure or a dispel with something to take off, Stamina below its base (a signed compare, `slt`), or any other effect. Refused, it says why and uses nothing ([items](items.md#potions)). The importer writes `Throw` on the four flasks, `DrinkInTrek` alone on Stealth, which C-up's menu leaves out, and both on the rest. In a battle C-up's menu lists only those `CanDrink` allows, and drinking shows no number ([combat-actions](combat-actions.md#spells-potions-and-skills-in-a-turn)). 1.0's `DrinkPotion` doesn't ask `CanDrink` again: it goes by the kind (`+0x27`), the Antidote through `AddEffect`'s Remove Poison at margin 255, Restore through `Entity.CureWeakness` (`0x8006FDB8`), and a lasting potion already working says so (`+0x3C`) and does nothing; the port's asks, which comes to the same, as nothing changes between the menu and the drink. `CanDrink`'s refusals are "That potion cannot be used right now." (`0x800D3150`) for a potion that would do nothing and "That potion cannot be used in Trek mode." (`0x800D3178`) for the flasks, Aspect and Jumping.

| Healing, Curing | heal 15–25% or 40–60% of the maximum, drawn whole, worked in single precision and truncated (`HealByPotion`); none in the Shadow's battle |
| Stamina | cure Exhaustion, then Stamina to its base |
| Antidote | cure Poison (the original adds Remove Poison at margin 255) |
| Restore | cure Wraith Touch, Debilitation, Weakness, Exhaustion, Stupidity, Clumsiness (`0x800D34B4`) |
| Strength, Dexterity | the stat's base +10 for 648,000 ticks, not on the dead |
| Clarity, Charisma, Stealth | Loremaster, Diplomat or Stealth +3, which does nothing to a skill the character can't learn |
| Defence | protection +15 (the original adds it in `Combatant.TotalProtection`) |
| Fire, Inferno, Acid flasks | blast 4d6, 8d6, 2d6 (`FlaskDamage`, `0x80065270`, which rolls all three and takes one) |
| Sleep Gas | blast of 15 from Stamina: no sleep and no duration |
| Acid | and corrosion: a 2d6 radius, each within it −(radius ÷ squared distance) protection until the battle ends |

**The blast** (`FlaskExplosion`, `0x800652F8`): each living combatant at a squared distance d from the landing cell, **kept in a byte** (so it wraps at 256) and at least 1, within the damage takes the damage ÷ d, left at 1 at the least if it isn't the target; friendly fire happens. The target then takes its share again as the blow lands (`Combat.EndCast`), so twice ([combat-actions](combat-actions.md#spells-potions-and-skills-in-a-turn); the port's line and number give both). The port keeps the damage and the byte, the user's choice. Acid's corrosion goes on those within its radius instead of the damage, stacking; those beyond it within the damage take the damage. The blast goes over the battle's slots (`Combat+1`), skipping the dead but not the fled. The target's share kills it at once when it's at least the hit points it leaves (`CheckDeath`, `0x80065E98`, after `TakeDamage`), before the blow takes it again: a rat of 31 is killed by a share of 16, with 15 left, and lives through 15 until the blow. The port's check is the same ([combat-parity](combat-parity.md#the-formula-vectors)). The blast reads no resistance: it calls nothing between the roll and `Character.TakeDamage` (`0x8006553C`) that does, so a flask has no element and a Fire Flask burns a Salamander's Fire ×0, in the port too. **The throw** (`ThrowChance`, `0x80064EFC`), the last flask of a stack, which the original never bursts and the port does (the user's choice), and the rest of a flask's turn are [combat-actions](combat-actions.md#spells-potions-and-skills-in-a-turn)'s.

In the port, the throw rolls the `blast` dice of every thrown potion's record, in the records' order, and keeps its own, as the original's `FlaskDamage` rolls all three, so the RNG stays in step. The flask then lands through `EffectLanding` on the one it hit (`Battle.FlaskExplosion`), and its blast and corrosion burst in `IMagicHost.Burst`, corrosion as a `protection` of −1 a level at the radius ÷ d.

**Neither the Defence potion's protection nor Acid's corrosion goes on the sheet's protection byte.** They're potion records, and `Combatant.TotalProtection` (`0x8005F570`) adds them each time it works out a total. It walks the seven potion records (the entity's `+0x38`), takes off each corrosion record's power (kind 3), and adds 15 if a Defence record (kind `0x0F`) is there. Then it adds the sheet's byte, body armor and a fifth of Warrior, and the total is 0 if it comes to under 1. The port does the same: a potion's `protection` effect skips the sheet (`EffectRules.Start` and `End`), and `Battle.Protection` adds `Character.PotionProtection`. On the byte, corrosion would wrap it: a Giant Rat with 3 protection and 5 corrosion would have 254 and could barely be hurt.

## Gear

- **Worn enchantments** go on with the armor, shield or gear (`Character.PutOnEnchantment`, `0x8007192C`, from `Entity.ApplyItem`, `0x8006F2A8`), never running down, and come off with it (the first of the spell's that never runs down). A dispel can take one off until the battle's end puts it back. Only three exist: the Haste Amulet (Haste 1), the Boots of Speed (Haste 5) and the Ring of Healing (Photosynthesis 1, margin 1). The ring heals its margin each time trek ages the effects, once a drawn frame (`EffectRules.Round`'s case at `0x80070BF8`, skipped when `World+2` says night): about a hit point a frame.
- **1.0 loses them on a load.** `PakSave.ReadPartySheet` (`0x80019498`) builds the sheet and puts on what's worn, enchantments with it (`PakSave.ReadSheet`, `0x8001928C`), then writes the 15 saved effects over the sheet's slots without looking (`0x800194D4`–`0x800194F8`). The save holds none, since it's written from the kept copies of the sheets (`Party.CopyToKept`, `0x8001844C`), which copy the gear as bare items, so the enchantments are gone. They come back when the item is taken off and put on again, or at the next battle's end (`Combat.Exit`, `0x80023060`, runs `Entity.ClearEffects` on each member). So a Ring of Healing worn at a load heals nothing until then. The port puts them on as loading puts the gear on, the user's fix ([below](#where-the-port-differs)).
- **Charges** go only from wands and scrolls worn as gear; the spells of weapons, armor, shields and other gear never run out. Nothing recharges them (`Party.RechargeItems`, `0x80078D5C`, is empty), neither camping nor waiting: in 1.0 the Stormdrake Claws cast Lightning ten times on their 5 charges, and a Lightning Wand loses one a cast. It reads as meant: the item info shows Charges: on a wand alone ([pause menu](pause-menu.md#the-party-tab)), so the player never sees other gear's, which look left from before only wands spent them. Batteries are [items](items.md#equipment)'.
- **Resistances** on the body armor, shield, weapon and the 12 gear slots count with the character's own (`Equipment.Resistances`).

## The AI's values

`SpellValues.Value` (`EnemyAI.SpellValue`, `0x80057498`): the record's `appeal`, or with none the highest the loaded spells give its first effect's type; and 0 when its type finds nothing to do: a `cure` with no candidate under what it cures (`0x8005B734`), a `charm` or `banish` whose filter allows no candidate (`0x8005B60C`, `0x8005B6AC`), `partOfDay` unless it changes the part of the day and the balance favours it (+1 for each combatant the light now hinders on the caster's side, or doesn't on the other's, −1 for the rest; `0x800615B4`), a `dispel` unless the school's timed records harm its side or help the other's more than not. The values range from 0 (Escape, Teleportation, the moon and sun, the morale spells) to 85 (Darkness, Light): a spell for its own side 51–85 (Darkness 85, Haste 73, Mirror 72, the `vs.` spells 71, the shields 61, the stat spells 51–56), a debuff or hold 31–43, damage 11–22, which is why enemies buff first ([combat-ai](combat-ai.md#the-casters-spells)). The conditional types read set B's candidates as it's built from the cell scored.

**What the AI may cast** (`Combatant.AICanCast`, `0x80060A94`, inside `CanCast` for a combatant the AI plays), for every source, an item's too: the Stamina for the cost at the casting level, a charge on an item's, the target's requirement and the part of the day. It never asks for a reagent: `CheckReagent` skips an enemy or an AI-played caster.

## The original's quirks the port keeps

The user chose to keep these, which look like bugs, as the original has them:

- **Spirit and Starlight Shield leave protection behind.** They add 2 a level but take off 1 a level as they end (`0x800707EC`), so each cast leaves a level of protection on the character until a load rebuilds the sheet. It's a parameter a mod can change (`ProtectionEffect.Leaves`).
- **Wind on an ally raises Stamina and its maximum for good.** It adds 2 a level to Stamina's base and modified values (`Entity.AddStaminaEffect`, `0x80072158`), and nothing takes it back, so maximum hit points rise with each cast until a load. It's a parameter too (`StaminaEffect.RaisesMaximum`).
- **A blocked charm or hold still hurts.** When the disable limit stops a charm or an immobilise, `ApplySpellEffect` logs that the target resists but returns 0, not −1, so `CastSpell` still rolls (margin) d6 of damage at the target (`0x800646E0`, `0x800648CC`). `Battle.Cast` returns that damage.
- **The dead keep their buffs.** `Entity.SubStat` (`0x80072450`) does nothing to a dead character, so when death takes a spell's or potion's effects off, a raised stat stays raised. Endurance's and Stamina's raise the maximum hit points too, until a load.

## Where the port differs

- **The Stamina spell raises Stamina** by 2L as it lands and takes it back as it ends, as its description says. 1.0's lowers it: `Character.AddEffect`'s case for kind 32 jumps into the weakening kinds' path (`0x80070560`, −2L), and `EffectRules.End` takes 2L more, so each cast leaves Stamina's base and maximum 4L lower until a load. The user chose the fix; the formula vectors list it as a known difference (`EffectsVectorTests`, `CastingVectorTests`).
- **The enemies may hold the whole party**, the user's choice. 1.0 runs the disable limit whatever the caster's side (`Combatant.CheckImmobilisingLimit`, `0x80063B9C`, through `DisableLimit`, `0x80063A00`, which counts the held and charmed in the enemies' slots): while three enemies are held or charmed and reserves are to come, an enemy's hold on a member is refused as resisted, and rolls the margin's d6 instead. It reads as a check meant for the party's casts reaching the enemies' too. The port limits the party's casts alone (`CastingVectorTests` lists the difference).
- **Wind does what its description says**, the user's choice: on an ally it holds the battle's weather clear while it lasts, the drawn fog lifting at the fog's rate, and lets the weather's own back as it ends ([the table](#what-each-spell-does)). 1.0's left the weather alone while it lasted and set fog as it ended, so a throw took fog's penalty after a Wind even in clear weather. Several Winds hold it together (`Battle.HoldWeatherClass`, counted). The permanent Stamina raise is 1.0's, kept.
- A charm takes whoever it's cast at. 1.0's `Entity.AddEffect` asks again what target set B's filter asked (`Ids.IsElemental`, `0x8006E110`, for Control Elem; the Marquis; the Zombies) and adds the record without the charm when it fails, which only a cast outside the filter, a mod's, reaches.
- One record can hold several effects; the original keys one per kind. The base game's records hold one each but Web of Starlight's (immobilise and 2d6) and Tap Stamina's.
- Any spell, potion or flask can carry any effect type ([How effects land](#how-effects-land)); the original's code carries each instant one for its own kinds only. The base game's records hold them where the original has them, so nothing it plays changes.
- Trek's ageing runs every tick, and what the effects do each round once a 30 fps frame (`Cadence.FrameTicks`), as the original does once a drawn frame.
- A worn enchantment is on after a load (`Equipment.Restore`), where 1.0's is lost until the item is put on again or a battle ends ([above](#gear)). The user chose the fix: 1.0 restores other state badly on a load too.

## Not settled

Nothing in the spell record. The ROM's `unk1E` bits 0x20, 0x40 and 0x80 are read nowhere in 1.0: `Spell.Init` (`0x8007A910`) copies the byte whole to the RAM spell's `+0x20` (`0x8007A9E4`–`0x8007A9F0`), and every read of it tests 1, 2 or 0x10 alone ([db](../formats/db.md)). The plugin doesn't carry the byte; `hbht db dump` keeps it.

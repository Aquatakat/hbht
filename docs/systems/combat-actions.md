# Combat: attacks, spells and what's drawn (2.5, 2.6)

What a combatant's action does in a [battle](combat.md)'s turn, and what the battle draws of it: an attack, from its targets to its blow; spells, potions and skills; the spells' visuals; and what the bodies hold. Read from 1.0's code and checked by running the original's own functions on the combat checkpoints' RAM and by the replay of the user's logged battles ([combat-parity](combat-parity.md)). Casting's rules, and where spells', potions' and flasks' effects land, are [magic](magic.md)'s; the enemies' choices are [combat-ai](combat-ai.md)'s. Addresses are 1.0's, and the names are in the symbol map.

## An attack

**Target set A** (`Combat+0x4E64`; `TargetSet.Rebuild`, `0x80069264`) is built at a turn's start, as the combatant moves (`UpdateFromBody`), as an aim is put away and by other callers; while a player aims (states 9, 10, 18) it isn't rebuilt on idle polls. `Rebuild` empties it, finds the candidates (`FindAttackTargets`, `0x80069304`: everyone in play `Combatant.CanAttack`, `0x80060590`, allows; one may be chosen), selects the first, and keeps the cursor on the last target (`+0x10`), else where it was (`RestoreCursor`, `0x8006A05C`). Without an action, and not healing, it stays empty.

- **`CanAttack`**: never the Shadow (entity `0x0226`: the target's entity against the entity list's byte at `0x800E28EC`, its place `0xAC`, plus `0x200`, at `0x800605A8`–`0x800605D4`); not healing, a target on the other side, not dead or gone, while the attacker has an action; its weapon reaching (`CanReachWithWeapon`): a flask always; melee and healing within reach (`IsWithinReach`, the bodies' radii plus 0.75); ranged from distance 3 to the weapon's range, truncated, with no opponent within reach; melee-or-ranged anywhere in range. Then **`ArenaGrid.WalkClear`** (`0x80067E6C`) over the grid between the two cells, or failing that **`ArenaGrid.HasSight`** (`0x800684C4`) from the attacker's body lifted by both heads' heights to the target's body's centre: the two lifts (the entity's height less the body's radius) are worked out for each, but the second block, pasted from the first with only its registers changed, adds the target's to the attacker's point too (`0x8006079C` and `0x800607DC`, both to `sp+0x24`; the target's vector at `sp+0x60` is never lifted), so the line comes steeply down to the target's middle and a body just before the target blocks it. `CanCast` lifts the target's own point. The port runs it head to head ([Where the port differs](#where-the-port-differs)). The line is a sphere of 0.1 swept through the collision, see-through triangles included, and no other living body within its unscaled radius of the line, which runs on past both ends.
- **`WalkClear`** steps the line's longer axis cell by cell: each must be inside, not taken unless the end is a step from it, and a step from the last unless the end isn't. A step is a rise under 2 in the grid's half units (`IsStep`, `HeightDifference`). But it steps only toward +X or +Z: going the other way its loop never starts and the line is clear unchecked, so a target with a smaller X (or Z, for a line longer in Z) never needs the 3D test.
- **The action** (`+0x25`, `Combatant.SetDefaultAction`, `0x800672D4`): no weapon, none, so **unarmed party members can't attack**; range 0, melee; else ranged, or melee-or-ranged for a Breath, a Spit or Spikes (`Weapon.AllowsMelee`, `0x8007B730`). Enemies have weapons too: a rat's is Rat Bite (`aidyn:weapon/0x0702`).

**Aiming** (state 1's A, `Combat.InputTurn`, with the stick centred and R let go): set A is built again, and with candidates `TargetSet.BeginAttack` (`0x800694A8`) orders them (`OrderByAngle`, `0x8006975C`) and puts the cursor on the last target, else where it was, else the first; state 9, the camera given to the cursor's body. A bow, a thrown weapon that flies or a flask (`+0x8A`, `Combatant.UpdateDrawnFlag`, `0x80066A2C`, through the bow list `0x800E0C08` and the thrown list `0x800E0C14`) takes state 18 instead, a free cursor at the target. The order is by where each body stands from the attacker's against its body's own heading field, the opposite of where it looks: ahead and to one side by the sine's size, descending; then behind and the other side, ascending; then ahead and the other side, ascending; then behind and the one side, descending (`Vec2.Cosine`, `Vec2.SignedSine`, a selection sort whose ties go to the later). It sets only the cycling order and the cursor's place.

**State 9** (`Combat.InputAttackTarget`, `0x800842C4`), each poll, edge-triggered, with no repeat:

- **Start** pauses, and the pause comes back to state 1, the camera to the combatant.
- **B, Z or L** put it away (`Combat.ReturnToTurn`, `0x80083C70`): state 1, the healing modes off, the focus on the combatant, both sets built again, the camera back. The body stays turned.
- **A** confirms (`ConfirmAttackTarget`, below).
- **The stick or the D-pad**, left, steps the cursor +1 through the order, and right −1 (`0x80084420`), the camera to the new target and the attacker's body turning toward it 10° a frame (`Combat.AimAtCursor`, `0x80084334`).
- **The camera**: with the cursor's body over 5 from the attacker's, the eye's goal is 4 behind the attacker, away from it, and 1.5 up (`CombatCamera.PullBack`, `0x8008D22C`), with `Camera::Mode1`'s last flag clear, which skips its rise, its leash and its rescue.

**State 18** (`Combat.InputThrownTarget`, `0x80084BA0`): C-left and C-right step the candidates; the stick moves a free cursor 0.8 a frame, on the frame's first pad event only (`Combat.Controls`, `0x80083B00`, hands each event its place in the frame), whatever the stick's size past the dead zone (a tenth a way) and whatever the frame's ticks, turned by the camera (`Combat.StickToArena`, `0x80084570`) and kept on the grid and within the weapon's range, and set A selects the candidate whose body is nearest it, within a unit (`0x80084A24`); A confirms the same way.

**The turn to face** (`ConfirmAttackTarget`, `0x80084078`): the target's slot goes in `+0x14`, set B is emptied and A keeps its selection but loses its candidates, the camera goes back to the attacker, its facing points at the target and its body's turn starts (`Combatant.FaceCombatant`, `0x8005FD28`), state 6. State 6's input (`Combat.InputFacing`, `0x80085018`) snaps the heading to the goal on every poll while the body's turning flag (`0x20`) is set, so the 10° turn never plays: **state 6 takes two frames whatever the angle**, then `Combat.StateFacing` (`0x800221B0`) carries out the attack.

**`Combatant.ExecuteAction`** (`0x80065F6C`), in order: a performance ends; the target is the last (`+0x10`); set B is emptied; the attack is resolved (`ResolveAction`, `0x80065ACC`: melee, ranged, or for melee-or-ranged melee if `CanAttack` holds as melee; then the attacker's request by its weapon's animation byte, `Combat.AnimationByWeaponClass` `0x800D27E0`: 0 → 16, 1 → 17, 2 → 13, 3 → 12, 4 → 15, 5 → 14, else 18); the Goblin Ambush's hold on Alaron (`Combat.ShadowFightTrick`, `0x80065DCC`, [combat](combat.md#the-special-battles)); **`CheckDeath`** (`0x80065E98`) at once, before any hit point goes: a blow that takes them queues "%s killed" (`0x50`) and calls **`Combatant.Death`** now ([combat](combat.md#deaths)), or with a Cheat Death on the target queues "%s cheats death" (`0xFC`); the attacker's moves set to 1; **the damage stored on the target** (`+0x29`, a byte); the camera's glide from the attacker to the target (`CombatCamera.StartGlide`); and state 2 with `ActionActive`. The acted flag stays down (only `PrepareCast` sets it), so a weapon's spell rolls its own margin.

**Melee** (`Combatant.MeleeChance`, `0x800622C4`, every step truncated to 16 bits):

- The attacker's modified Strength and Endurance, base Level, the weapon's hit, 6 × its weapon skill (the modified one, `Skills.WeaponModified`) and 2 × Warrior.
- Less the target's 2 × modified Dexterity, base Level and shield defence (`Combatant.ShieldDefence`, `0x8005F6B0`: the shield's defence × the shield skill + Warrior, a byte, so Warrior counts without a shield). The Dexterity and shield don't count against a target held fast (`IsImmobilised`).
- 20 more from behind, and Thief × 10 (`SkillTerm`, `0x80062ED8`).
- × 1.15 (`0x800D2964`) against a target with no Stamina; the height, c + h × 0.05 × c, with h the attacker's cell's height byte less the target's (`ArenaGrid.HeightDifference`, `0x80067AD4`, on the cells as the records last read them from the bodies; `0x7F` off the grid); × 0.8 with no Stamina of its own; × 0.9 at night; the aspect (`AspectScale`: a Lunar attacker × 1.25 at night and × 0.75 by day, a Solar one the other way round, `0x800D2990`); the Troubadour (`TroubadourScale`); at least 5.
- **From behind** (`Combatant.IsBehind`, `0x8005FB54`): the point the distance away along the attacker's facing lies within half a unit of the target in X and Z (`IsAimedAt`, `0x8005FA44`), and the two facings' dot product, rounded half away from zero to six places, is at least 0.7071.

**`ResolveMelee`** (`0x800628A4`): a d100, which `AdjustRoll` (`0x80062594`, magic's) turns with Warrior: at or under min(Warrior, a fifth of the chance), a second d100 comes off it. A hit is the adjusted roll under the chance.

- **A hit** draws the damage, then a d100 for the weapon skill (`Combatant.GainSkillExperience`, `0x80062650`: at or under a tenth of (8 − skill)² for a skill under 6, else 5, the base trains a level; half the points on a miss). It shows and queues "%s attacks %s" (`0x24`) or, the roll adjusted, "%s scores a critical hit on %s" (`0x2C`), and queues "%s loses %d hit points" (`0x30`; `0x34` for 1).
- **A miss** draws the experience, shows "attacks" and queues "%s avoids the attack" (`0x20`).
- So a melee hit draws the roll, [the adjustment], the dice, [the weapon's spell], the experience. **A critical is only its line**: the second d100's bigger margin is its only effect.

**`MeleeDamage`** (`0x80062AE4`): (the weapon's dice + the Strength tier) d6 (`Combatant.StrengthTier`, `0x80062008`: the first of 4, 9, 17, 24, 28, 32, 36, 39 at `0x800E0C30` the modified Strength is at or under, else 8); plus the margin's step (`SkillCheckStep`, `0x800DA348`) and the attacker's base Level; less protection (`ProtectionVsAttack`, `0x8005F71C`: `TotalProtection`, `0x8005F570`, the sheet's, body armor's and a fifth of Warrior, with the potions' ([magic](magic.md#potions-and-flasks)), 0 if under 1, plus the shield's unless from behind, a byte); plus Thief × 2 from behind; at least 1. Then **`ApplyResistance`** (`0x80062704`): the product of every resistance of the target's own and its gear's that meets the weapon's element (`ResistanceMultiplier`, `0x80061AA4`, in order: the combatant's two entries at `+0x30`, copied from the entity, then the armor's, the shield's, the weapon's and each worn item's `+0x20` record; an entry of element 0 never applies; `ResistanceApplies`, `0x80061A30`: the element, Physical also Cutting and Smashing, Magic every element but Physical, Cutting, Smashing and Holy), truncated, at least 1 unless one is 0; a weakness over 1 makes the morale check due of a target the AI plays (its record's flag 8; `0x800627A4`), as a spell's damage does ([magic](magic.md#casting)). So a Holy sword hurts a Wraith or Lugash, Physical and Magic ×0 alike. Then the weapon's spell (`Combatant.WeaponSpellProc`, `0x800627D0`, [magic](magic.md#casting)). The sheet's own damage byte (`CharExp+3`) and the entity's damage aren't read.

**Ranged** (`Combat.RangedChance`, `0x80062F50`): the attacker's modified Intelligence and Dexterity, base Level, the hit, 6 × the skill and 2 × Warrior; less (distance − 5) past 6; less the target's base Level, and unless it's held, its 2 × Dexterity and shield defence; 20 from behind (no Thief); × 1.2 against a target that hasn't moved or acted (`+0x22` 0), × 1.15 against one with no Stamina, × (h × 0.05 + 1), × 0.9 with no Stamina of its own, × (1 − the rain's intensity × 0.3), × 0.7 at night, × 0.8 against one that has moved; the aspect, the Troubadour, at least 5. `ResolveRanged` (`0x80063324`) is `ResolveMelee` with no Strength dice and no blow from behind in the damage (so the shield's protection always counts), the experience after the lines, and "%s misses %s" (`0x38`). No ammunition is spent. `Combat.WeatherPenalty` (`0x80062D78`) is `ThrowChance`'s, not this; `Combatant.HeightModifier` (`0x80062E0C`) has no caller.

**The action's states** (ticks are the frame's delta; the action timer is `Combat+0x52CC`):

- **State 2** (`Combat.StateAction`, `0x80021774`) waits for the blow (`Combat.StepActionTimer`, `0x80021708`: the action timer, counting every frame's ticks, at 30 or more **and** the attacker's body's `+0x18` 0, a signed halfword). Then an arrow or thrown weapon flies (below), or the blow lands: with damage, `Combat.ApplyHit` (`0x80021930`) takes it through `Character.TakeDamage`, sets the target's request 7 and shows the number over its head (`Combat.ShowDamage`, `0x80021294`); with none, an opponent dodges (request 8) and an ally is helped (10, with healing's number); a dead target falls (`PlayDeathAnimation`, request 9 at once). Then state 4, with the queued line shown (`FlushQueuedLog`). The attacker's swing plays on meanwhile.
- **The strike** is when `+0x18` goes to 0, not the swing's end. `ProcessPlayers` (`0x80012784`) sets `+0x18` to the body's request (`+0x14`) as the actor enters a state, and also (`0x80013108`–`0x800132D0`), for a body playing an attack (`+0x18` 12 to 18), on the tick its frame before the step is `(u16)(int)(length × share)` in single precision: the share is the entity's (`EntityBody.Strike`, `0x8006C7FC`: its row of the model table, `CodeTable.EntityModels` `+0xC`, 0.2 to 0.67, or 0.4 at `0x800D2CF8` without one), or 0.4 for a bow's or a thrown weapon's requests 14 and 15 (`EntityBody.ShotStrike`, `0x8006C884`, `0x800D2CFC`). The request is 0 by then (`Combat.AfterFrame` let it go after its first frame), so the blow lands at the next check. The attacks are each a single Borg7 state with one branch, any request back to idle, so nothing else is keyed to them. Counted from the request's frame, the target reacts at the first frame at or past `max(30, H + 2)` ticks, H the strike frame; at 2 ticks a frame Alaron's Short Sword lands at 32 (state 39, 71 ticks, 0.42, H 29), a rat's bite at 66 (279 ticks, 0.23, H 64) and a goblin's Sabre at 82, where the swings end at 72 to 280. Checkpoint 10's rat is at frame 51 of its 279 with `+0x18` still 16, before its strike at 64. An entity whose actor has no branch for its weapon's request (the Goblin Sergeant `0x0216`'s Short Bow, 14) never takes it, `+0x18` stays 0, and the blow comes at the 30 ticks.
- **State 4** (`Combat.StateResult`, `0x80021F30`): `Combat.RemoveFallen` (`0x80021D2C`) waits for the target's body's `+0x18` to be 0 and 60 ticks (a rat's fall is 239), then frees a dead one's body (`Combat.FreeBody`, `0x80021C6C`: its effects and potions off, its numbers gone, its portrait skulled, the body's fade started, `Combat.Bodies[slot]` emptied, the record kept; [the fade](combat.md#deaths)), gives the camera back to the attacker and deselects the target. With no target left, the turn ends: the next begins as the body starts to fade, and nothing waits for it.
- **The one-shot requests**: after every frame `Combat.AfterFrame` (`0x80023360`) zeroes each body's request but 0–3, `0x14`–`0x16` and `0x19`, so a request combat makes is asked for through every tick of its frame (two at 30 fps), and `ProcessPlayers` copies the request to `+0x18` whenever the actor enters a state (and at an attack's strike, above), so `+0x18` goes back to 0 when the reaction ends and the actor idles. A dead body (`+0x18` 9, its request no longer 9) holds its last frame (`+0x77C`), where `+0x18` takes 0. That's `ProcessPlayers`' rule for every body, which cut-scenes' actors play by too ([scripts](scripts.md#actors)).

**Arrows and thrown weapons** fly as the spells' cast scene (`Combat.CastScene`, `0x800DB0C8`, state 3). After the swing, `Combat.StartCastCamera` loads the arrow (Borg5 `0x348F`) for a bow, else the weapon's own model (`Combatant.LoadWeaponScene`, `0x80066CF8`), from the hand's locator (`SceneGetLocatorPos`, `0x8009ECD0`, of `+0x88`, as the body was last drawn; with no such locator nothing flies, and the blow lands all the same) to the target's middle (its feet plus half its height), or a flask's to the target body's centre (its sphere's, one radius above its feet). The held one is freed as it leaves. It goes in a straight line in 3D, its length L (`Combat.MissileLength`, `0x800DB0B0`), `v` a tick × the frame's ticks, raised by sin(travelled / L × π) × `arc`, with `f = muzzle + (L ≤ 40 ? L/40 × 4 − 2 : 2)`, `v = f > 10 ? 0.5 : f ≥ 1 ? f × 0.05 : 0.05`, `a = 10 − muzzle + (L ≤ 40 ? L/40 × 8 − 4 : 4)` and `arc = a > 10 ? 7 : a ≥ 1 ? a × 0.7 : 0.7`, the muzzle number by weapon (`Combatant.WeaponMuzzle`, `0x80066DD0`, the combat record's `missiles`). It's scaled as the attacker and turned along its way (`Combat.DrawCastScene`, `0x80021300`): rolled about its own X by its angle (`Combat.MissileAngle`, `0x800DB0C4`: the muzzle's turn, 90 for the Javelin, plus its spin a tick, a flask's 8), which `Scene.SetRotationRPY` (`0x8009DA48`) gives `guRotateRPYF` as the roll, then by `guAlignF` with no twist to the line's direction (`Combat.MissileStep`, `0x800E6870`), so its −Z points along the line and its Y as near up as the line allows: a flask tumbles end over end toward its target, leaving the hand at the top of the throw. It leaves at the throw's or the shot's strike (state 2's check, above), partway through the animation: a Goblin Poisoner's Poison Dart (request 15, state 9 of actor `0x302A`, 199 ticks, 0.4, H 79) at tick 82, not 200. The blow lands once it has arrived **and** the action timer, which `Combat.StateCast` (`0x80021B24`) counts from the launch through the flight, has reached 30 (`Combat.EndCast`, `0x800219D8`): on arrival for a flight of 30 ticks or more, a dart's of 5 or more units. The Goblin Ambush's darts are the four Goblin Poisoners' (entity `0x022A`, script `0x0365`): each leaves the hand partway through the throw and arcs to Alaron, who reacts as it lands. **`CombatAttackFx.Add` (`0x80089B80`) has no caller** in either build.

**The numbers over heads** (`CombatStatIndicator`, `0x8008A000`–`0x8008A8D4`): a pool of 16. Each is its number (a byte, 1–3 digits) put into a 64 × 16 texture from the digit textures (Borg1 22, 17, 21, 20, 15, 14, 19, 18, 13, 16 for 0–9, upside down), centred, at the body's X and Z and its head's height (half a unit higher for healing), and stays there. It's a billboard of 0.5 × 0.2 (healing 1.5 × 0.6), growing by s × scale across and s × 0.4 × scale up and rising s × 0.5 × scale, with s = sin(t / 120 × 90°), negative for healing, and scale its distance from the camera × 0.15; coloured (255, 255, 128) for damage and (128, 128, 255) for healing, its alpha `(u8)(base − (s8)(s × 255))` from 255 (0 for healing); gone at 120 ticks. Nothing is shown for a miss, a critical or a death.

They're drawn as the shadows are (`Plane.SetDrawState`, `0x8004C350`, with flags 9, then `Plane.Draw`, `0x8004C4A4`, from `CombatStatIndicator.Frame`), after the world and its bodies (`Combat.DrawFrame`, `0x80021564`, after `HandleZoneEngineFrame`). The state list it calls (`0x800DFCC0`) sets one cycle, `TEXEL0 × SHADE` for colour and alpha (`G_CC_MODULATERGBA`) and **bilinear filtering** (`G_SETOTHERMODE_H` `0xE3001201 00002000`, `G_TF_BILERP`); nothing on the numbers' path sets the filter again. The texture is IA8, 64 × 16, its intensity and alpha alike, with no mip levels (`G_TL_TILE`), S wrapped and T clamped (`Scene.SetTextureTiles` given `0x1000`). Flag 1 turns on the z-buffer and the pixel's depth (`0x200005`, `G_ZS_PIXEL`), with `G_RM_ZB_XLU_SURF` (`0x504A50`): blended, tested against what's drawn and not written, and without fog. `CombatNumbersView` draws them filtered linearly, Godot's stand-in for the RDP's three-point filter ([rendering](rendering.md#whats-left)), without mipmaps and clamped (the texture's first and last columns are always clear, so wrapping S changes nothing). It tests them against the depth as the original does, without writing it, so a body or the scenery in front of a number hides that part of it.

**The target marks** (`CombatTargetMarks.Draw`, `0x80089830`) aren't a cursor. Every frame, in every state, each combatant selected in set A (set B in states 10 and 11) has its body's ambient light set to a grey of |sin(n × 3°)|, n its ticks marked (`Body.SetTint(body, 1.0, f, f, f)`, `0x8001371C`, which takes the tint's level to 1 at the next step): a pulse from dark to full once a second, ending at once when it's deselected (`Body.ClearTint(body, 0.0)`, `0x80013774`). So the selected enemy pulses in state 1 as soon as one is in reach. It's the ambient, not the lights: the sun still lights it over the grey ([What spells look like](#what-spells-look-like)). The tint's level is 1 throughout (checkpoints 04–06: level 1, the ambient the pulse's grey), so the moon's cyan light on the body is at full, (0, 193, 193) in the rats' battle's morning (moon height 0.966 in checkpoints 01–11, `Bodies.SetLights`, `0x80013960`–`0x80013a6c`), and on the dark side of the pulse there's little else. Like every light the engine adds, it reaches only the first vertex of each pair the RSP loads, the others taking the near-black ambient alone ([rendering](rendering.md#bodies-light)), so the original's dark rat shows only a little green. The port lights the same vertices (importer 59).

## Spells, potions and skills in a turn

Checked by running the original's own functions on checkpoints 05 and 07 and against 08, and in the logged battles.

**The held menus** (`CombatMenu`, `0x80087C60`–`0x80089780`). `Combat.Controls` gives each poll's event to `CombatMenu.Input` (`0x80087C8C`) before the state's own: in states 1, 9, 10, 11, 18 and 25, a C-up press opens C-up's menu (`CombatMenu.OpenItems`, `0x80087F2C`, state 23) and a C-down press C-down's (`OpenSpells`, `0x80088028`, state 22), C-up winning a poll that has both. Opening puts an aim away (`Combat.CancelToTurn`, `0x80083C2C`: `ReturnToTurn`, and `RevertAction` from 9) and stands the body. The menu is a `ChoicePopup` that isn't in the widget manager, so it comes and goes on the frame, with no fade. How it looks is [ui](ui.md#the-held-menus)'s.

- **Moving**: the stick's or the D-pad's up and down move the cursor a row, wrapping, held repeating after 30 polls and then every 5 (`StickRepeat`, `0x800AD708`). A, B, Z, L, R, Start and the C buttons do nothing.
- **Choosing**: letting the button go (`CombatMenu.Close`, `0x80088118`) sets state 1 and frees the menu, whose destructor calls the cursor's row's callback (`DestroySpellsMenu` `0x80088F90`, `DestroyItemsMenu` `0x800895A0`), so the callback's state wins. The row is remembered by party slot (`CombatMenu.SpellCursors` `0x800E3180`, `ItemCursors` `0x800E3190`, reset at the battle's start) and the cursor starts there next time, clamped to the list.
- **C-down's rows** (`CombatMenu.BuildSpellsMenu`, `0x80088B58`): Lay On Hands while modified Healer isn't 0 and modified Stamina is at least max(1, 11 − Healer) (`Entity.CanLayOnHands`, `0x80071ED0`); Heal With Herbs with a Herb in the party's inventory and Stamina at least max(1, 5 − Healer) (`CanHealWithHerbs`, `0x80071F38`); Perform while modified Troubadour isn't 0; then the spells known, sorted by name with `strcmp` (so the `vs.` spells, lower case, come after the rest), each listed if its reagent is in the party's inventory or it needs none, and not Photosynthesis at night. Nothing else is tested: a spell out of reach, too dear or for the field is listed and fails later. The skills are blue with the Healer's cross `0x0185` or the lute `0x018C`; the spells red with their school's icon (`Spell.SchoolIcons`, `0x800DEFC0`: Chaos `0x01A9`, Elemental `0x01A5`, Naming `0x01A6`, Necromancy `0x01A7`, Star `0x01A8`, none for the unschooled). No level, cost or count shows.
- **C-up's rows** (`BuildItemsMenu`, `0x800891B4`, from `ListPotions`, `0x80088190`): one row a kind of potion the party holds, in the kinds' order, that `Potions.CanDrink` allows now (so not Healing at full health; never a flask or Stealth: `ListPotions` skips kinds below 4 and kind 16; the port lists the potions whose record has `DrinkInBattle`, in the records' order, [magic](magic.md#potions-and-flasks)), green with the flask `0x35E5`; then the weapon's, shield's, body armor's and gear slots' spells with charges left (`CombatMenu.HasCharges`, `0x800885E0`), yellow with the item's own image.
- **Empty**, a message instead: "No spells or skills available" or "No potions or item spells available" (code strings), which releasing closes. Checked: the rows of every member at checkpoint 07.

**What a row does** (its callback):

| Row | Sets | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a spell, an item's spell | the entity's selected spell (`+0x3D` the source: 1 known, 2 armor, 3 shield, 4 weapon, 5 gear; `+0x3E` the place or slot) | `Combat.BeginSpellTargeting` (`0x80083ECC`): a placed area (the spell's `unk1E` bit `0x10`) state 11, else state 10 with `TargetSet.BeginSpell` |
| a potion | `Combatant.SetSpellAction`: the action 3, the old one kept (`+0x24`), the potion's kind (`+0x27`), a performance stopped, healing put away | state 9 with set A holding the drinker alone; A drinks, B, Z or L put it back |
| Lay On Hands, Heal With Herbs | the healing flag (8 or 9, `SetHealingMode`), a performance stopped | state 9 with set A's healing candidates |
| Perform | nothing | state 25, set A holding the caster alone |

**The selected spell** persists: `Combatant.Init` (`0x8005EAE0`) selects the first spell a combatant knows as each is made, and a menu's choice changes it. `Combat.EndTurn` puts the cast's margin (`+0x3C`) to `0xFF` but keeps the choice; a refusal for the reagent, the Stamina or an item's charges (`Combatant.FailSpell`, `0x800636A4`), a spent scroll or wand and an equipment change with a spell (`Entity.ApplyItem`) clear it. Set B is built for it at every turn's start and as the combatant moves, so its reach tints the portraits red whether or not a menu opens.

**Target set B** (`TargetSet.Rebuild` in mode 1; `FindSpellTargets`, `0x800693C4`): every combatant `Combatant.CanCast` (`0x80060C38`) allows, and as many may be chosen as `SpellTargetCount` (`0x80060818`, the port's `Casting.TargetCount`) gives: every combatant in play for an All spell (then every candidate), (level + 1) >> 1 for a ByLevel one, else one. `CanCast`:

1. Never by or at the Shadow (the caster's and the target's slots, `+0x20`, against `Combat.ShadowSlot`, `0x800D94CC`, at `0x80060C70`–`0x80060C88`; `Combat.CreateBody` sets it for place `0xAC`, compared at `0x8005C528`); `SpellCanTarget` (`0x80060988`, the port's `Casting.CanTarget`): alive, not gone, on the caster's side for a Party spell and the other for an Enemy one (Area and All either, so Fireball takes the party too), not under the spell already, its requirement met, and `SpellKindAllowsTarget` (`0x8006088C`, the record's filter: the Elementals for Control Elemental and Banishing, no Chaos for Charming, the Marquis, the Zombies); an AI's own tests (`AICanCast`, [magic](magic.md#the-ais-values)).
2. Teleportation and a Self spell take the caster alone.
3. Its reach: the effective level × the spell's range byte, from the caster's place, to the target's body, its scaled radius added (`InSpellRange`, `0x80060B60`, the port's `Casting.Reach`); a placed area's is the level alone, from the placed point (`Combat+0x18/+0x1C`). Sense Aura has none.
4. Its line: `WalkClear` from the caster's (or the point's) cell, or the same cell, or `HasSight` from 2.0 above the floor under that cell (`0x800D28A0`, a sphere of 0.5 swept from 50 to −10) to the target's head, only the target's body excluded. A 2-cell caster's own footprint blocks the walk toward +X or +Z ([combat-ai](combat-ai.md#stuck-and-resting-for-no-visible-reason)).

`Rebuild` selects in slot order. A menu's choice calls `TargetSet.BeginSpell` (`0x80069574`) instead: a spell of one target orders the candidates by angle as an attack does and starts on the last target if it's one, else the first of that order (the camera on the first); one of several selects them all if they're fewer than it takes, else the nearest to the caster's body (`SelectNearest`, `0x80069D90`, a later equal first), and the player chooses nothing. **Checked**: 07's set B for Rheda's Endurance (ByLevel, level 4) is the four members as candidates with her and Alaron, the two nearest, selected; and every member's spells give the candidates 1.0's `BeginSpell` gives.

**State 10** (`Combat.InputSpellTarget`, `0x800844BC`): Start pauses back to state 1; B, Z or L put it away; left steps a single target's cursor +1 and right −1, wrapping (`TargetSet.StepSpellOne`, `0x800696D4`), the camera to it and the caster's body turning to it (`Combat.AimAtCursor`); A is `Combat.ConfirmSpellTarget` (`0x80083F58`): set A emptied, set B's candidates cleared, the **highest** selected slot the first target (none: back to the turn), the camera to the caster, the margin 0, and the caster turns to it (state 6) or, casting at itself, state 6 at once.

**State 11** (`Combat.InputAreaTarget`, `0x80084964`), entered with the centre at the caster's place: the stick alone (not the D-pad) moves it 0.8 a frame, as state 18's cursor, up being the camera's way (`Combat.MoveAreaCursor`, `0x8008465C`), on the grid and within the range × the level of the caster's body, and set B is built again about it; state 11's camera is 8 and 16 about the centre. A is `ConfirmAreaTarget` (`0x80084184`), as state 10's, but Teleportation's point must be clear (`Combat.SpotIsClear`, `0x8005D474`: no living body's centre within its radius and the caster's), or nothing happens. `Entity.Teleport` (`0x80071978`) reads the point itself.

**The marker** (`Combat.DrawTargetCounts`, `0x80021448`, every frame) draws one translucent cylinder, scene `0x0797`, at the focus, set on the floor by a sphere of 1 swept down, scaled (r + 0.2, 2, r + 0.2) at the scene colours' alpha 64 (`CombatSpellFx.DrawNumber`, `0x8008ABD8`): red with r the reach about the caster in state 10, red with r the level about the point in 11 (1 for Teleportation), green with r 1 at a flask's aim in 18. It draws no numbers.

**The cast** (`Combatant.ExecuteAction`'s spell branch and `Combatant.CastCurrentSpell`, `0x80065C08`), a target at a time: set A's selection cleared; `CastSpell` at the target ([magic](magic.md#casting)), the first preparing it and the rest sharing its roll, reagent and cost (the acted flag); a damage of 0 shows "%s casts %s" and queues "resists" (`Combatant.LogResisted`, `0x8006382C`, which `CastCurrentSpell` hands the target for both names at `0x80065C84`, so the line names the target as the caster: "Air Elemental casts Wind"); the caster's body plays request 5 and `CombatSpellFx.Start` (`0x8008C43C`) its visual, even for a refusal; then as an attack, `CheckDeath`, the damage on the target, the glide and state 2. State 2 waits for the caster's request 5 to end and 30 ticks, then the target is hit (request 7 and the number), or a same-side one helped (10) and an opponent dodges (8); state 4's `RemoveFallen` takes the target off both sets and, with more selected, turns the caster to the **first** selected by slot (state 6), until none is left and the turn ends. **A spell never goes through state 3**, which only arrows, thrown weapons and flasks take. A cast whose margin is below 0 (refused or failed) casts at no more targets for a player. The lines: "%s casts %s" shown, "%s cast on %s" queued and flushed at state 2's end; the failures shown and queued; a requirement's refusal its own line ([magic](magic.md#casting)). **Checked**: replaying 07's A gives 08's state 2, target, margin 4, acted flag, lines and two draws, then Alaron with no draw.

**Potions and flasks** (`Combatant.ThrowFlask`, `0x80065600`, action 3):

- **A potion** is drunk (`Combatant.DrinkPotion`, `0x80064C84`, [magic](magic.md#potions-and-flasks)): a heal says what it gained (shown and queued) or that it failed, Stamina that it's full, the rest that it took; no number shows. The body plays request 5, one is used up and the action goes back. **What a heal says it gained is the share rolled, not what it gave back.** `Combatant.HealByPotion` (`0x80064BBC`) puts in the line whatever `Entity.HealPercent` (`0x800715DC`) returns: the rolled percentage of the maximum, before `Character.Heal` caps it at full. Alaron at 28 of 30 who drinks Healing gains 2, but the line can say 5. Only a share of 0 says it failed.
- **A flask in hand** is action 3 with the kind and `+0x8A`; it stays in the party's inventory. It's taken in hand from the pause menu's Party tab (below), which ends the turn and stows the weapon (`Party.StowHeldItem`, `0x800763B0`, at the turn's end): the member is left unarmed. Each turn's start lets it go if the party has none left.
- **A spell cast with a flask in hand does no damage**, to friend or foe. `Combatant.ExecuteAction` stores the cast's damage on the target (`+0x29`) only when the caster's action isn't 3: `0x800660A8` branches past the store at `0x800660B0`. The dice are still rolled, and the effects that land in `ApplySpellEffect` still land, so only the damage is lost. `Combat.EndCast` (`0x80021A2C`) then takes the cast for a throw and bursts the caster's `+0x29` as a flask of its `+0x27` kind. That damage is 0 unless something left it set. With no damage on the target, an ally plays request 10 (helped) and an opponent request 8 (the dodge). Play reaches this: `Combatant.RevertAction` (`0x8006679C`) keeps action 3 at the end of each turn while the party still holds that kind of flask, so a member who takes a flask in hand, or throws one with more of its kind left, casts damage spells for nothing until the stack runs out, a potion is drunk, or a weapon is taken in hand.
- **Aiming** is state 18 with set A: the other side, any distance (`Combatant.WeaponReach` is 400).
- **The throw**: `ThrowChance` (`0x80064EFC`): the thrower's modified Intelligence and Dexterity, base Level, 6 × Thrown, 2 × Warrior and 40, less half the target's shield defence, its modified Dexterity and base Level; less (distance − 5) past 6; 20 from behind; +10 at a target with no Stamina, −20 at one that has moved or acted, −25 having moved; the night's −30 and fog's −10 or a storm's −20 with the distance again (`Combat.WeatherPenalty`, `0x80062D78`, on `World+4`, [weather](weather.md)); the aspect and the Troubadour; at least 5. A d100 that Warrior turns. A hit draws 4d6, 8d6 and 2d6 always and takes its own (`FlaskDamage`, `0x80065270`; Sleep Gas 15), shows and queues "%s attacks %s", keeps the damage on the thrower (`+0x29`) and the target's cell (`Combat+0x5444`), and trains Thrown on a d100; a miss queues "%s misses" on half the training. Either way request 15 and one used up (`ConsumeFlask`, `0x80064AB0`, which puts the action back): the next of the stack stays in hand, and **the last empties the hand at once, so it never flies or bursts**: its target dodges.
- **The flight** is an arrow's (state 3), scene `0x34C5` to the target's feet, the muzzle number 4 and a spin of 8 a tick.
- **The blast** (`Combat.EndCast` → `Combatant.FlaskExplosion`, `0x800652F8`) about the landing cell, its numbers shown at once, is [magic](magic.md#potions-and-flasks)'s; the target's loss is queued and its death checked. Then `EndCast` lands the target's share as a blow: **the target takes it twice**, and the number and the line show it once. A Fire flask of 10 takes a rat from 31 to 21, then 11, and shows 10. Acid thrown and missed corrodes about the last flask's cell (`Combat+0x5444`; read, not run).

**The Troubadour** (state 25, `Combat.InputTroubadour` `0x8008508C`: no text, set A holding the caster; A is `Combatant.StartTroubadour`, `0x800662F4`): with a performance already going in the battle, "%s is already performing" and the turn goes on; with no Stamina, "%s is too weak to perform"; else (Troubadour × 5 + Intelligence) × 2 against a d100: made, the bonus (`Combat+0x13`) is the margin's step, at least 1, 3 Stamina go, "%s performs" and the notes over the performer's head ([What the bodies hold](#what-the-bodies-hold)); failed, "%s fails to perform". Then request 5 and state 26, 120 ticks, and the turn ends. The skill trains on a d100 at or under a tenth of (8 − skill)² (8 from 6), halved on a failure (`GainSmallExperience`, `0x8006620C`). The performer's turns say "%s continues to perform", and it pays a Stamina point each round's end. A performance ends as it acts, changes weapon or action, takes a potion or healing in hand, is engaged or dies, or with no Stamina at a round's end (`StopPerforming`, `0x80066100`, which takes the bonus back).

**The Healer** (`Combatant.ResolveHealer`, `0x8006591C`): set A's healing candidates (`CanAttack`'s branch): the caster's side, hurt, within melee reach and sight; Lay On Hands never the caster, Heal With Herbs the caster with no test. The Party tab's own rules: Lay On Hands with Stamina over 2, for 3, heals Healer × 2 + 5; Heal With Herbs takes a Herb first, then for max(1, 5 − Healer) Stamina rolls a d100 under Intelligence × 4 + Healer × 11 and heals 4 × the step. Healed: request 5, "%s heals %s" shown and queued, "gains %d hit points" queued, and the healing number (`+0x2A`) after the target's helped request; else "%s fails to perform healing". No skill experience. In the Shadow's battle it's barred ([combat](combat.md#the-special-battles)). Restore is the pause menu's Healer popup alone, which doesn't open in a battle ([ui](pause-menu.md#the-party-tabs-actions)).

**Sense Aura** (`Entity.SenseAura`, `0x80070070`) puts up the Party tab's message box with code strings by the cast's level: the target's name; from 1 "Leader" or "Follower"; from 3 "%d Total Hitpoints"; from 5 "Level %lu"; from 7 its resistances, "Immune to %s" at 0 or less, "Resistant to %s" under 2.0, else "Vulnerable to %s"; from 9 "Total Armor Protection %d". **Checked** at level 9 on the Tomb Rats' leader (setup 14, played without a log): "Leader", "44 Total Hitpoints" (Endurance 15, Stamina 25 and Level 4, `Character.MaxHp`), "Level 4", "Resistant to Fire" for its ×1.5, and "Total Armor Protection 3", its sheet's alone, all as the port's. "Hitpoints" is the one place the game doesn't write "hit points"; the bugfix mod is to patch the combat record's `text.aura.hitpoints` ([ADR 0029](../adr/0029-content-fixes-wait-for-a-bugfix-mod.md), roadmap Phase 5). It holds state 4 from ending the turn; any press closes it and ends the turn at once (`Combat.DismissPopup`, `0x80083E30`).

**Escape** (`Combatant.Leave` on each target while the party can flee) takes a member off the field as a fleeing enemy goes; its body gone, state 2 waits for it for ever, a softlock (by the code and 1.0's own functions run). Only cheating reaches it: Ardra, who knows it, never fights, and its scroll isn't in the game.

**Teleportation** (`Entity.Teleport`, `0x80071978`, `Character.AddEffect`'s kind 12, from `CastSpell` and so before the caster's request 5 and its visual) reads the focus, the point state 11 placed (`Combat+0x18`, `+0x1C`), truncates it, and moves the caster only if its footprint there is free on the grid (`ArenaGrid.IsOccupied`, the caster's own cells still taken; a cell with no floor is taken too, so a point off the arena's floor spends the cast and moves no one, with no line). Then the combatant stands at the point (`Combatant.SetPosition`), its field goes (`TurnOrder.StopBody`), and its body is put at the point plus its scaled radius in X and Z and set down from on high (`Combatant.PlaceBody`, `0x8005FE04`), facing as it was. The camera, the turn's start and the grid aren't touched: the next read of the body (`SyncFromBody`) takes the footprint from the point and puts it where the body stands, so the cells it left stay taken until a path is found (`RefillWithBodies`). The visual is the caster's actor `0x1E50` alone, at the body's new place; the cast does no damage, so state 2 plays the caster's helped request (10). `Battle`'s `IMagicHost.Teleport` ports it.

**The pause menu's Party tab** in a battle (`PartyMenu.InventoryA`, `0x80035FA0`, by `PauseMenu.Member`, [ui](pause-menu.md#the-party-tabs-actions)): a weapon equipped (`Party.EquipWeapon`, `0x800758CC`, then `Combatant.AfterEquipChange`, `0x800610C0`: performing stopped, healing put away, the weapon's action, moved) or a flask taken in hand (`Party.EquipFlaskInCombat`, `0x800753F0`) ends the turn as the menu closes (state 19, `Combat.StateEndTurn`); a flask in hand shows in the silhouette's weapon slot, crossfading as it's taken (`DollEquipmentMenu::GetEntityItemId`'s weapon slot, `0x8003E66C`, gives the Fire Flask's `0x1000` while the member's combatant, found by `PartyMenu.CombatantOfEntity` `0x8003E5DC`, has action 3; the port's `CombatPause.Held` gives the one held, the same icon in the base game), though the weapon stays worn until the turn's end stows it. A scroll goes into a gear slot and a weapon comes off with no turn spent.

## What spells look like

Read from 1.0's code (`combatspellvisuals.cpp`, `0x8008AD60`–`0x8008D1DC`; the debug build names `ProcessSpellVisuals` and the file), with its pools checked in checkpoints 08–12 and `Start` run on 07. There are **no status icons**: `combatstatindicator.cpp` is the numbers alone. What players remember as icons cycling over heads, "brief, then a long gap", is the lasting scenes' turns below. The six one-line functions at `0x8008AA20`–`0x8008AA84` are this module's entry points, not the stat indicator's.

**What each spell and potion shows** is data, on the spell and potion records' `visuals` and the magic record's `refused` and `resisted` ([hardcoded-content](../hardcoded-content.md)), from five `switch`es on the kind with jump tables, alike in all three ROMs: the caster's actor (`CombatSpellFx.CasterActor`, `0x8008CB40`), the target's (`TargetActor`, `0x8008CCE0`), whether the target's stands at the head (`TargetAtHead`, `0x8008CCA8`: the charms, the controls, Debilitation, Stupidity, Weakness, Exhaustion, Clumsiness and Crushing Death), the lasting scene (`AuraScene`, `0x8008CE78`), and a potion's actor and lasting scene (`PotionActor`, `0x8008D038`; `PotionAura`, `0x8008D0FC`: Acid, Strength, Dexterity, Defence). Each actor asks for a memory size, and is skipped when the heap's largest block is smaller, which with the Expansion Pak's 4 MB free never happens. Detecting Traps, Poison, the two Detects and Stealth show nothing; Banishing's target plays Escape's actor; a refused cast plays actor `0x1CAA` at its caster and a resisted one `0x37F1` at its target, whatever the spell (`AddVisual`, `0x8008B8D8`, `0x8008B8E0`).

**A party member's Wraith Touch crashes 1.0.** The caster's actor, `0x1EF7` (scene `0x1EEF`), has four emitters. Three sit on locator 4 of the caster's body, which `AttachScenes` attaches. The fourth sits on the target's locator 3 and aims at the caster's locator 4. The Wraith's actor (`0x3434`, locators 0, 3, 4 and 5) is the only body with a locator 4. When the actor's request 5 starts its particles, `ParticleHead.Start` calls the emitter's callback at once (`0x800AA11C`), and the callback reads the locator (`0x800A9910`). `SceneGetLocatorPos` (`0x8009ECD0`) finds -1 at `+0x1D4 + 4n` (`0x8009ED14`) and calls `Debug.RaiseError` (`0x8008E4F4`). The error thread then runs the crash screen (`CrashScreen.Run`, `0x80005138`): "Manual Crash", with the last `gGlobals.text`. Play doesn't reach it. Only the Wraith knows Wraith Touch. The Wraith's and the Marquis' weapons cast it as an enchantment, at the party. Its wand (`0x0D15`) and the scroll that teaches it (`0x1105`, which the data names "Teleportation") are in no shop, loot table, map or script. The port's emitter ends without the locator, so a modded Wraith Touch plays without the caster's stream.

**A failed cast is a refused one.** `ResolveCastRoll` (`0x80063DF4`) returns 0 for a roll at or over the chance, after "%s fails to cast the spell" (`Combatant.LogCastFailed`, `0x800637C0`, shown and queued) and the reagent given back; `AttemptCast` (`0x80063F7C`) and `PrepareCast` (`0x8006406C`) pass the 0 on, and `CastSpell` (`0x800648CC`) returns `0xFFFE` for it, as for a spell unavailable, a requirement unmet, a reagent missing or Stamina short. `CastCurrentSpell` (`0x80065C08`) turns `0xFFFE` into −2 for `Start`, so every one of them plays actor `0x1CAA` at the caster, with the caster's request 5 and the spell's own sound (`Player.SetCastSound` comes first), and no glow, no dimming and no lasting scene. State 2 then plays the target's helped request (10) for one on the caster's side or its dodge (8) for an opponent, as for a cast that did nothing (`Combat.StateAction`, `0x80021774`, on a damage of 0). Actor `0x1CAA` is particles alone: its scene (`0x1CA2`) has no models, and request 5 takes it to state 6 (99 ticks), which starts three emitters at its locator 4, at the caster's feet. Each puts up a burst of 12 after 0, 15 or 30 ticks and then one every 3 ticks for 38 to 46, over about a second and a half; they drift up, swell and fade from orange, yellow and pale blue to violet, teal and purple: the puff of coloured smoke players remember as the spell fizzling. The resisted cast's `0x37F1` (scene `0x37E9`) is the same at the target, its puffs falling and sliding sideways, green and yellow fading to amber and lilac, into the ground before the colour has fully turned.

**Starting** (`CombatSpellFx.Start`, `0x8008C43C`, from `CastCurrentSpell` for each target, after `Player.SetCastSound`): a cast entry in pool A (64, `0x800E3218`, whose index is the handle), a body entry in pool C (16, the original's "players", `0x800E3220`) for the caster and the target, and in pool B (128, `0x800E321C`) the visuals: a refused cast (result −2) the refused actor at the caster; a resisted one (−1) the resisted actor at the target; any other, damage 0 included, the caster's and target's actors and the target's lasting scene, which joins the turns (`QueueAura`, `0x8008B498`); then **the sky dims** to half over 30 ticks for 150 and back over 30 (`DimSky`, `0x8008AECC`; `Sky.FadeBrightness`, `0x8001DE88`, the brightness at `0x800D9518` that multiplies every sky colour and the backdrop), **both bodies glow** white (`GlowBody`, `0x8008B364`: `Body.SetTint(body, 30.0, 1, 1, 1)` for 150 ticks, then `ClearTint(body, 30.0)`, the body's fog off meanwhile), Darkness and Light ease the sky's time of day to midnight or noon over 60 ticks (`World.SetTimeOfDayGoal`, `0x8007C450`, an offset at `world+0x28` that only the sky, the sun and the moon read; the moon's odd place in it is the original's too), and Wall of Bones, Frozen Doom and Web of Starlight hold their target's idle pose (`HoldBody`, `0x8008B3F0`, body `+0x70E`: `ProcessPlayers` doesn't step an idle body's animation while it's asked for nothing; the held body's idle stops, its lasting scene takes its turns, and it moves again as the hold ends). A potion drunk or a flask's blast on a body (`Combatant.AttachSpellVisual`, `0x80064B10`: a heal that took, the Stamina, Antidote and Restore potions, a lasting potion that took, each body a blast hurts or corrodes) sets its request to 5 and plays the potion's actor and lasting scene on it (`StartPotion`, `0x8008C664`): no glow, no dimming, no sound. `CastCurrentSpell` files the handle in the target's first record of the spell without one (`+0x14`), when the cast did damage or added an effect, and `AttachSpellVisual` in the first potion record of the kind without one (`+0x08`).

**An actor** stands at its owner's feet, the body's centre less its **unscaled** radius (`+0x64`), raised by the entity's height for one shown at the head, followed while its animation plays; it's never turned or scaled. It's asked for request 5 from its first frame, and ticked a tick at a time until its state's animation has run its length (119–401 ticks), then left where it was until its emitters' particles have all gone (`Particles.SceneHasEmitters`), when it's freed with its emitters stopped. Its scene's `+0x2A4` is the body it sits on and `+0x2A8` the other of its cast (`AttachScenes`, `0x8008BB08`: the caster's for the caster's actor, the target's for the target's; a potion's both the drinker), which its emitters' locator bits 7 and 8 name ([particles](../formats/particles.md)), so a Fireball's particles fly from the caster's locator 3 to the target's. It's tinted by the screen's fade (`Scene.SetTint(F, F, F, 255)`) and lit by its own scene's lights, under the combat camera. Nothing ends an actor early: a body that dies or leaves is only marked (`ClearForBody`, `0x8008C880`), and its visuals play on where they were.

**The lasting scenes take turns, one at a time across the whole battle** (the port's options can show each character's at once instead: [Where the port differs](#where-the-port-differs)) (`ProcessSpellVisuals`, `0x8008BCA0`; `NextAura`, `0x8008B510`): a translucent shell or ring at its body's locator 3 (the head), unturned and unscaled, loaded as its turn comes (5 frames after the last load), whose turn is a hump of alpha sin(t / 125 × 3.14) × 255 over 125 ticks (`AuraLength`, `0x800E3234`) times its material's own alpha; then the next body in pool C order that has any waiting takes its turn, or the same body, each body's in pool B order by a cursor of its own. **Every cast holds the turns back for 300 ticks** (`CastWindow`, `0x800E323C`): the playing hump's clock doesn't move, and the alpha's factor falls to 0 over 30 ticks and comes back over 30 after (`AuraFade`, `0x800E3230`, cos of a ramp). So nothing shows for 5 seconds after a cast, and with *n* lasting effects in the battle each shows for 125 ticks once every *n* × about 135: one buff alone pulses every 2.2 seconds; four buffed members each show 2 of every 9. There are no fixed slots. Only effects a cast or a potion in this battle made show: not gear's, nor those on before it began. **Checked**: 08's pools hold Rheda's cast with its lasting scene loaded and held at alpha 0 in the window; 09–12 show the turns passing between Rheda's and Alaron's scenes.

**Ending.** A record's end (`EffectRecord.FreeVisual`, `0x8007AB9C`, and the potion's `0x80079DB8`, from `Entity.RemoveEffect`, only in combat) calls `EndEffect` (`0x8008C7A4`): Darkness and Light ease the time back over 60 ticks, a hold lets its pose go, and the lasting scene is asked to stop, which it does once its turn's hump is over; the actors play on. A cast entry goes once its three visuals have (and for Darkness and Light, once their effect has), with its body entries' references (`Housekeeping`, `0x8008AF1C`). A second cast of an effect already on the target files no handle, so its lasting scene has no record to end it and takes turns until the battle's end. `Combat.Exit` frees everything (`CombatSpellFx.Free`, `0x8008C8E4`): every body's glow back over 30, poses let go, the time offset back in a tick. None of the pools is checked for room.

**No visual moves the camera or plays a sound.** The cast's sound is the caster's: `Player.SetCastSound` (`0x80065BFC`, only from `CastCurrentSpell`, so a refused cast too and never a potion) sets body `+0x72A` to 5 and `+0x72C` to the spell, and `AudioKeys.PlaySpellCast` (`0x800534BC`) plays the spell's sequence on the first tick the body's request is 5, as a body key does, then clears both. No combat file calls an audio function: a battle's sounds are the music, the weather and the bodies' keys, the weapons' among them.

**The bodies' light in a battle** is trek's (`HandleZoneEngineFrame`, `Bodies.SetLights`, [rendering](rendering.md#bodies-light)): the ambient, the sun, the moon, then the map's lights for the party's members, of which the Battle arenas have none. Only combat changes a body's tint (`+0x75C` level, `+0x760` rate, `+0x764` colour), which moves its ambient from half the sky's colour toward the tint's colour and scales the moon's cyan light: the target marks ([An attack](#an-attack)) and a cast's glow.

## What the bodies hold

In a battle each body holds its weapon and shield; in trek no body holds anything but what a script shows ([scripts](scripts.md), `ShowItem`), and nothing else in 1.0 writes the bodies' attachments. Read from 1.0's code and checked against the Combat checkpoints' bodies.

**A body's attachments** are three records of 12 bytes at body `+0x730`: a u32 Borg5 (0 for none), the scene once loaded (`+4`, 0 until the first draw loads it), and a u16 locator of the body's actor (`+8`). `AttachItemToPlayer` (`0x80014BA0`) frees the slot's old scene and sets the Borg5 and the slot's first locator (`Body.AttachmentDefaultLocators`, `0x800CDC0C`: slot 0 locator 0, the hand; slot 1 locator 2, the shield arm; slot 2 locator 3, the head); `ChangeAttachmentNode` (`0x80014C6C`) sets another; `FreeAttachmentFromPlayer` (`0x80014C2C`, `Body.FreeAttachment` `0x80014B04`) queues the scene to be freed (`Combat.FreeQueue`, `0x800D96B0`). Slot 0 is the weapon, the arrow or the flask; slot 1 the shield or a bow; slot 2 the Troubadour's notes. `FreePlayer` frees all three with the body (`Body.FreeAllAttachments`, `0x80014B48`).

**What goes in them** is `Combatant.EquipVisuals` (`0x80066AEC`): slots 1 and 0 freed, the locators set to 0 and 2 (`+0x88`, `+0x89`), then:

- **The arm** holds the shield (`Combatant.ShieldModel`, `0x80066844`: the entity's armor slot 1, its model by `ItemTables.ShieldModels`), unless the entity's record has the shield skill 0xFF (`Combatant.CannotUseShield`, `0x80066C18`: Brenna, Donovan, Godric, Keelin, Niesen and Sholeh), or the shield has no model.
- **The hand**, by the action (`+0x25`): nothing for none (0); the flask's scene (`0x34C5`) for a flask or a potion in hand (3), whichever it is; else, with a weapon (the entity's `+0x24`, the one the sheet wields, not `+0x84`), its own model (`Combatant.WeaponModel`, `0x800668B4`, through `ItemTables.WeaponModels`, `0x8006CDD4`; none for a bite or a claw). A spell changes nothing: the caster keeps its weapon.
- **A bow** (`Combatant.WeaponIsBowKind`, `0x80066908`, the list at `0x800E0C08`) puts the arrow (`0x348F`) in the hand and the bow in the arm, in place of the shield: the bow at locator 1 and the arrow at 0, or the other way round for the eleven whose `+0x84` is 0 (`EntityBody.Hand`, `0x8006C8BC`, a switch on the entity list's place: the bandit bosses and woodsmen, Becan and Keelin).
- **The hand stays empty** for the eight whose models have their weapons (`Combatant.HasNoWeaponAttachment`, `0x80066C5C`, the entity list's places at `0x800D2A0C`: Shamsuk, the Gorgon, the Chaos Lieutenant, the Harpy, the Marquis, the Minotaur, the Minotaur Lord and Niesen, whose staff has a model all the same). It's tested after the arm is filled, so their shield or bow shows.

`EquipVisuals` runs as a combatant is made (`Combat.CreateBody`'s `SetDefaultAction`, the reserves' too), as a flask or potion is taken in hand (`SetSpellAction`) and put back (`RevertAction`), as a turn starts with a flask the party has no more of (`TurnOrder.BeginTurn`), as the pause menu changes a weapon (`Party.StowHeldItem`, then `AfterEquipChange`), after `EnemyAI.SwapWeapon`, and as an arrow, flask or thrown weapon lands (`Combat.EndCast`, `0x80021AE8`): `Combat.StartCastCamera` (`0x80023434`) emptied the hand as it left. A shield can't be changed in battle (`Party.TakeOffShield` refuses in state 14). Nothing breaks: no weapon or shield wears out. Death, flight and the post-battle windows leave what's held on the body until it's freed.

**The Troubadour's notes** go on slot 2 as a performance starts (`Combatant.StartTroubadour`, `0x800662F4`: `AttachItemToPlayer(body, 2, 0x1ABB)`, locator 3) and come off as it stops (`StopPerforming`, `0x80066100`, which `FreeAttachmentFromPlayer`s slot 2, so they're gone the next frame). Borg5 `0x1ABB` is one billboard card of music notes, not a lute: 1.64 square, standing on its foot, unlit, with white vertices, blended, its texture (Borg1 `0x1ABA`, 32 × 32, scroll byte `0x01`) moving a texel toward +S every second draw. Neither function plays a sound, tints the body or starts a particle; the performer's request 5 is the one-shot every cast plays, and no later turn asks for another. Every party member's actor has a locator 3, so any member can show them. Their size, height and scroll match the emulator's.

**Drawing** (`RenderPlayers`, `0x80013DCC`, after the body's own scene): each slot with a Borg5 loads its scene the first time (`BorgAnimLoadScene`, then `Scene.UseOwnMatrix` `0x8009E3B4`, so it's evaluated from its own matrix, and `Scene.EnableFog` `0x8009E590`), and is skipped if the body's actor has no such locator (`Scene.HasLocator`, `0x8009EAE8`). Its matrix is the locator's (`SceneGetLocatorMtx`, `0x8009EB10`, set with `Scene.SetMatrix` at `0x80014260`): the locator model's rotation (`guRotateRPYF` of its Borg2 rotation, [scenes](../formats/scenes.md)), then its position, then its transform as the animation poses it, so the item turns, moves and scales with the hand, the body's scale with it, and isn't turned any more for a shield or a bow. Slot 2 alone (`bnel $s6, 2` at `0x80014290`) is then raised 1.0 (`0x3F800000`) in the world's Y (`Scene.Translate`, `0x8009D970`, which adds to the matrix's translation), whatever the body's scale. A word at `0x800DA490` doubles every attachment's size (`Scene.ScaleMatrix`, `0x8009DC6C`), as its neighbour `0x800DA494` does the heads': nothing writes either by its address, so they're likely the `!bigw` and `!balloon` cheats' ([hardcoded-content](../hardcoded-content.md)), which the port doesn't have. It's lit as the body (`Bodies.SetLights`, whose locator argument is never read: the lights are found from the body's place) and drawn at the body's alpha, so it fades with a body that flees; but its prim colour (`Scene.SetTint` at `0x80014254`) is the scenery's tint times the screen's fade, where the body's own scene keeps white. Then `Scene.Draw` (`0x80097244`), the path every scene takes, so an attachment's billboards face the camera's place (`BorgAnimDrawSceneRaw`) and its scrolling textures move once a draw. A billboard's size is its transform's scale times the scene's scalar (`SceneData.scalar`, which `Scene.SetScale`, `0x8009DBCC`, sets for the body's own scene), and nothing sets an attachment's, so the notes are 1.64 across on any body: the body's scale only moves where the head is. They stand from about 1 to 2.6 above the head's locator. Its look is its Borg5's alone: no glow or texture by the item.

**The port** keeps the slots in the core (`Combatant.Held`, `HeldModel`, the notes `Combatant.NotesScene` and `NotesRaise` in `NotesSlot`) and draws them in the runtime's `BodyView.Hold`: each scene on its locator's attachment node, the notes raised after, with its own copy of its scrolling textures (`ScrollHeld`, at the textures' frame from `Game.ScrollTextures`, as the scenery's: the original's once a drawn frame would be quicker at 30 fps), its billboards turned to the camera with the scene's scalar left at 1 (`FaceHeld`, `Billboards.Face`'s `sceneScale`), and the scenery's tint (`TintHeld`, from `CombatantView.Draw`); the screen's fade is the runtime's overlay.

Of the entities that carry a weapon, only Zaratas has no locators, so draws nothing; Brenna, Donovan, Godric and Niesen have no locator 2 (none of them can use a shield), and Jundar, Yuli, Edur, Phelan, Pandara, Txomin and Yeraza carry shields their actors have no arm for.

## The port

The classes are in [combat](combat.md#the-port)'s table: `Battle.Attack.cs`, `Battle.Strikes.cs`, `TargetSet`, `ArenaGrid`, `MissileFlight`, `CombatNumbers`, `TargetMarks` and `BodyTint` for an attack; `Battle.Spells.cs`, `Combatant.Spells.cs`, `Battle.Skills.cs`, `Battle.Magic.cs` and `Ui.CombatMenu` for spells, potions and skills; `SpellFx` for the visuals; `Combatant.Action.cs` for what the bodies hold.

### Traps

- **The blow lands at the strike**, not the swing's end: `AtStrike` before each body steps, `AfterBodies` letting `Body.Playing` (`+0x18`) go there, the share `Battle.StrikeShare`'s: the entity's `strike`, or for requests 14 and 15 its `shotStrike` and `throwStrike`. The missile leaves at the same check.
- **A weaponless action**: the original's `CanReachWithWeapon` takes a melee or ranged action without a weapon away (`+0x25` to 0) as it tests it; the port's leaves it, since it reaches no one either way and everything after treats an action without a weapon as none.
- **Seven of the visuals' actors are particles alone**: the refused and resisted casts' (`0x1CAA`, `0x37F1`), Dragon Flames' target's (`0x1C55`), Banishing's and Escape's target's (`0x1C9F`), vs. Star's and Wraith Touch's casters' (`0x1E9D`, `0x1EF7`) and the Fire Flask's (`0x1A56`). Their scenes have no models and every animation of theirs holds still, so their glTFs have no animations (the still ones are in the extras, [animation](../formats/animation.md)) and Godot gives them no animation player: `ActorAnimator` runs such an actor without one, its states timed by the still animations and its emitters started by its requests. Their glTFs have no geometry and so no binary chunk, which Godot's reader warns about, and `Models` would make such a scene a bare node without the locators its emitters start at, so `GlbFile.LoadNodes` builds their nodes from the glTF.

### Where the port differs

The user's choices:

- **An attack's line of sight runs head to head**, where 1.0's lifts the attacker's point by both heads and aims at the target's middle (`Combatant.CanAttack`'s pasted block, [above](#an-attack)).
- **A flask's target shows and says all it lost**: the blast takes its share and the blow takes it again, as 1.0's do, but the queued line and the blow's number give both (`Battle.FlaskExplosion` returns what the blast took, which `Land` adds to the number), so a rat of 31 hit for 10 is left with 11 and shows 20. Sleep Gas's blast drains Stamina, which hit points count, so its target shows 30. 1.0 and 1.1 show half of what was lost; the user kept the damage and fixed the display.
- **The aiming cursors' frame is 4 ticks** (`Cadence.AimFrameTicks`), 0.2 a tick, for state 18's free cursor and state 11's area: the Combat checkpoints' frames were 2 to 4 ticks (`Frame.Delta`, `0x800D8094`), 4 in both aiming states sampled, and at the port's usual 2 the flask's circle went twice as fast as the console's and was hard to steer.
- **A healing potion's line says what it gave back** (`Battle.Skills.cs`, `EffectLanding`'s `Healed`), where 1.0's gives the share it rolled before capping at full.
- **A spell cast with a flask in hand does its damage**, where 1.0's `ExecuteAction` drops it.
- **The last flask of a stack flies and bursts** as the others do: the port leaves a thrown flask in hand until the turn's end puts the action back, where 1.0's `ConsumeFlask` empties the hand as it's thrown.
- **A cast that does nothing names its caster**: "Alaron casts Wind", then "Air Elemental resists", where 1.0's names the target as the caster.
- **The Healer refused in the Shadow's battle says why**: Common line 437 stays shown and queued (`HealingBarredLine`), where 1.0's `ResolveHealer` overwrites it the same frame with "%s fails to perform healing".
- **Escape doesn't softlock**: state 2 goes on to the result when its target has left the field, a caster that has left still casts at the rest of its targets, and with every member able to fight gone the party has fled (state 20) at the next turn's change.
- **A hold doesn't lock the battle.** 1.0 locked once in a logged battle, in state 4 after Sholeh's Web of Starlight landed on a Tomb Rat ([combat-parity](combat-parity.md)). The likely cause, read from the code: `CombatSpellFx.Start` holds the target as the cast lands (`HoldBody`, body `+0x70E`), before its reaction; Web does no damage, so the target is asked for the dodge (8), which `Combat.AfterFrame` clears after a frame; `Bodies.Process` doesn't step a held body whose `+0x14` and `+0x16` are both 0 (`Body.IsHeld`, `0x80012754`), and `+0x16` is latched from the request only at the state's animation frame 1, 2 ticks in. So on a frame of delta 1 the target enters the dodge (`+0x18` 8) and freezes, and `RemoveFallen`, waiting for `+0x18` 0, never ends the turn; the web can't age off either. Any hold could lock so, though one usually doesn't. The port holds a request for the frame's 2 ticks (`Cadence`), so it never locks here.
- **The lasting scenes' turns** are the original's by default, one at a time across the battle, which may be its designers' way of keeping effects from crowding the screen. The options' Spell auras (`Settings.SpellAuras`, `SpellFx.Turns`, `AuraTurns`, which the session sets from each tick's `SessionSettings` and as the battle begins) can show each character's at once instead, each over its own head with its own hump and loading. The 5 seconds after a cast hold both. An enhancement as [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-the-port-fixes-bugs-and-sets-enhancements.md) has them.
- **A shot and a throw leave at the entity's own shares**, its body's `shotStrike` and `throwStrike` (`EntityBody`, `Battle.StrikeShare`), where 1.0 has one 0.4 for both and every entity (`EntityBody.ShotStrike`, `0x8006C884`). The importer leaves both at 0.4, so the base game plays as 1.0's; a mod can time a bow's or a thrown weapon's release to its own actor, as `strike` times a melee blow.
- **Kept as the original's**: Heal With Herbs in the Shadow's battle loses its herb before the bar refuses it, as a potion drunk there is used up for nothing; Acid thrown and missed corrodes about the last flask's cell; a flask's blast keeps its distance in a byte and reads no resistance ([magic](magic.md#potions-and-flasks)).

Not ported:

- An arrow, flask or thrown weapon leaves from the hand's locator as the runtime last drew it (`Combatant.LocatorAt`, which the runtime sets from `CombatantView.Locator`), but from the attacker's middle (its feet plus half its height) for an actor without that locator, where `Combat.StartCastCamera` flies nothing, and in a battle with no runtime.
- What a body holds is lit by the lights at the body's centre, not at its locator.
- Trek's bodies (a script's `ShowItem`) don't take the scenery's tint or face their billboards.

By the tick:

- The action's timers are counted and tested every tick, not once a frame, so where an attack's strike frame is odd its blow lands a tick sooner than at 30 fps (a goblin's Sabre at 81 rather than 82), and state 6's snap takes two ticks rather than two frames.
- The spells' visuals step every tick; their timers are the same at 30 fps, and a lasting scene loads 10 ticks after the last rather than 5 frames.
- Held, the stick and D-pad repeat an aim's cursor and a held menu's rows as the runtime's menus do; the original steps an aim once a press and repeats a menu after 30 polls and then every 5.

Others:

- A placed area confirmed with no one in it goes back to the turn, as state 10 does with none selected; the original's branch for it wasn't read.
- A cast beyond the visuals' pools shows nothing, where the original's reads past its free list; no battle comes near 64 lasting effects.
- Body armor or a shield equipped from the combat pause's Party tab is refused with "You cannot equip that item!": the original's equip table has no row for them, so it looks to refuse them so.

# Playtests

The user's notes from playing the runtime and timing the original, newest last. What they led to is in the [systems](systems/) docs.

- **The user's first walk (Sep 27, 2026).** The user walked from the spawn to Gwernia (M07), and the barn, statue and castle line up with their emulator capture. Their notes, in order of how much they stood out:
  - **Vertex colour looks missing** on some models, trees especially. The unlit primitives do carry `COLOR_0`, so check whether Godot's import applies it to textured unlit materials, and whether the `WrapMaterials` swap keeps it. The N64 material should take over either way.
  - **Alaron needs his round shadow** on the ground under him (the player shadow in `ZoneEngine.cpp`; see the combat visuals in [hardcoded-content.md](hardcoded-content.md)).
  - **The camera:** the generic orbit camera with mouse control feels good, but the SM64-style one is still the target. When a wall is behind the player, the spring arm pulls the camera into his face.
  - **Movement speed** is too fast now, but the original's is too slow and feels poor. Port the original speed exactly as the fidelity default and offer a faster profile (ADR 0006's fidelity settings; roadmap Phase 6). Gravity is probably Earth's, since 1 unit is about 1 m, and there's little falling in the game.
  - **Step height is too low.** He gets stuck on low edges. `CharacterBody3D` has no step-up, so either add one or port the original's collision response, which collides with edges and corners by the triangle flags.
- **The original camera, timed by the user (Sep 27, 2026).** In the emulator, on the 1.0 ROM:
  - It lags. After running left or right and stopping, it takes 500–1000 ms to settle.
  - It always wants to be behind Alaron. Standing still, it turns slowly toward his back and decelerates as it gets there. It took 45 s to go from about 5° off head-on to 90°, then another 70 s to settle behind him. Facing it head-on, it stays put, as if it can't decide which way to turn.
  - It pulls in toward Alaron when it hits collision.
  - Holding R and moving the stick orbits it. It won't go below level with Alaron, or above about 75°.
- **Movement, timed by the user.** From the statue in Gwernia to the tree north-northeast of it along the water, Alaron runs in about 9 s and walks in about 24 s.
- **The sky at night (Sep 28, 2026).** On the beach at L08 around 21:40, the user called bodies' lighting correct for now. A detailed pass comparing screenshots across the weathers can come later. Two notes:
  - **The clouds drift against the scenery** as the camera turns. Near the arch at the left of the screen, they moved along with the camera and slid past the arch. See [rendering](systems/rendering.md): the original places sky sprites at the sine of their angle, where perspective puts the scenery at the tangent, and the sky also read the camera a tick ahead of the drawn scenery. The clouds' grid of three repeating images makes it look worse, since a lookalike can take a cloud's place after a turn.
  - **The projected clouds still stretched toward the edges of a 16:9 screen** (Sep 29, 2026). The user saw the same in the emulator, only less of it at 4:3, so it's the original's. The projection had only fixed placement across the screen, at a level look, so the sky is now projected fully as directions (see [rendering](systems/rendering.md)).
  - **Neighbouring clouds parted as the camera turned** (Sep 29, 2026). A cloud whose trail touched another moved away from it when the pair came off the middle of the screen: the sprites were pinned only at a point and drawn at a fixed size. They're now patches of sky, projected corner to corner.
  - **The moon has no surface texture.** That's the original's: its six phase images are IA8 with every intensity nibble at F, so they're white discs shaped by alpha. The new moon's faint mottling is in its alpha.
- **Billboards and scrolling textures (Sep 29, 2026).**
  - **Billboards are faithful.** Bushes and Oriana's path lights face the camera at every angle as in the original.
  - **The path lights are tinted blue**, where the user expects bright white, and they should go out as the player reaches them. See [rendering](systems/rendering.md).
  - **Gwernia's sea scrolled two to three times too fast.** The port shared one texture between all the scenes that use it, where the original gives each scene its own copy. With that fixed the sea is too large to compare by eye, but the fire and the gore on the body outside Gwernia, where the console runs at about 15 fps, still scrolled about twice as fast. The user chose to scroll textures as if at 20 fps, the console's average. At 20 fps they match the original within a few percent.
- **The pause menu in the runtime (Oct 1, 2026).** The user opened the Party tab from the "Before final encounter" save.
  - **Feels right:** the top face button (Y) for C-down's details, and the shoulders (LB and RB) to change the member.
  - **Broken, fixed:** holding the stick left or right changed the list once a frame; the D-pad worked. See [ui](systems/ui.md#the-model-and-the-layouts).
  - **The original starts the tab afresh** each time the menu opens, remembering only which tab was open, so the presses to reach something become muscle memory (Start, member right, right, right, down, down, select, down to heal). The port now does the same.
  - **Fit and finish, for a later session:** the shield's icon on the silhouette is 2 pixels too high. And the animations, the list sliding in, the portraits moving and resizing and the details fading (see [ui](systems/ui.md#the-pause-menu), not ported yet), matter more than they seem: they draw the eye to where the controller has moved.
- **Fast-forward on the Talewok shore (Oct 4, 2026).** With a thumbstick held (160 ticks a second at the time; now 240), the framerate fell from about 120 to about 25 on the shoreline from Talewok toward Port Saiid, and came back to normal when the stick was let go. Not looked into. Something there costs far more per tick than elsewhere, which a higher tick rate shows. Worth a profile if it matters.
- **The credits and the save screens, timed by the user (Oct 17, 2026).** The port's credits ran in step with the original's, played side by side, so the scroll's half a pixel a frame is at 60 frames a second ([ui](systems/ui.md#the-credits)). The save and load screens' pages took a little over a second each to slide in the original, by stopwatch, with an ease-out; the user found that too slow and asked for half, which the port now does ([ui](systems/ui.md#saving-and-loading)).
- **The prologue, side by side with the emulator (Oct 21, 2026).** The user played from a new game through the Goblin Ambush, Oriana's hut and the camp south of the castle to leaving Gwernia, on the 1.0 ROM and the runtime at once. The prologue plays from start to finish. What differed:
  - **A new game doesn't show the opening cinematic** in the port.
  - **NPCs turn faster** in the port. The user finds the port's no worse, and the original's perhaps too slow.
  - **Battles** (Alaron against the Giant Bat, then Giant Rats) rolled different damage, as their random numbers differ, but nothing out of the ordinary; experience matched. Alaron's custom line in the Goblin Ambush sits better in the port's box than in the original's.
  - **The post-battle screens:** the training and loot screens play the area's ambient sounds, where the original's are silent; the training screen's left panel draws in the full-size font, where the original uses the small one; and Alaron's level gained there played no sound.
  - **Defeated enemies vanish** at once in the port. In the original they lie at the end of their death animation and fade out.
  - **The world map** doesn't show the forest and Gwernia, though the party has been to both.
  - **Oriana's hut is a little greener** in the original, the floor especially, and the cut-scene after it is lit differently: there the fog falls on the actors in its wide shot as on the scenery.
  - **Back in Oriana's hut,** Alaron faces Oriana in the original and the camera in the port.
  - **A seam in the forest (K08):** the original shows a crack of sky down the path where the port has none, and the port shows an edge of geometry, at the right, that the original doesn't.
  - **The minimap grows** in the original when the player stands still after reading a book or plaque, or after a cut-scene, as if B were pressed. The port's doesn't.
  - **The pause menu remembers the party member** last chosen in the original. The port goes back to Alaron.
  - **At night in Gwernia (M07)** the stars or drops in the sky sit in the same places in both.
  - **The camp south of the castle:** the original adds its journal entry ("I found a bizarre camp south of the castle…") as the party approaches it, and the cut-scene of the Chaos minions plays on leaving it. The port adds the entry only after the cut-scene.
  - **As before:** the forest's cut-scene with Yeraza no longer starts on the way out; the guard on the way out of the forest runs back and forth as he should; and turning differs from the original's but better, which the user keeps.
